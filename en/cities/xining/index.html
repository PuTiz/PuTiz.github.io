City Intro

History & Culture: Xining is the capital of Qinghai, on the northeastern edge of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau in the Huangshui Valley. It was a hub on the ancient Silk Road and the Tang-Tibet Ancient Road and has long been called the 'summer capital'. It is a multi-ethnic city where Han, Tibetan, Hui and Tu cultures meet. Xining is a city of many ethnic groups living together, where Han, Tibetan, Hui, Tu, Salar and other peoples coexist harmoniously. The Butter Sculpture Lantern Festival at Kumbum Monastery is a great event of Tibetan Buddhism, held on the fifteenth day of the first lunar month. Folk events such as the Tu Nadun Festival and the Hua'er Fair are rich and varied. Halal food culture runs deep, and Dongguan Mosque is the centre of Muslim prayer. Xining lies in Qinghai Province and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers of different periods.

Geography: On the northeastern Qinghai-Tibet Plateau in the Huangshui Valley, the city centre is about 2,275 m above sea level. It has a highland continental climate with cool summers, cold winters and a large temperature difference between day and night. Xining lies in Qinghai Province, where landforms, rivers and climate have shaped the city's layout. Summer (Jun–Aug) is cool and pleasant, a summer retreat; autumn (Sep–Oct) is colourful. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary can be arranged by area. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons; check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out. Roads and railways are the main connections, and major sights can be linked from the city or county town.

Highlights: See the butter sculptures at Kumbum Monastery, the rapeseed flowers at Qinghai Lake and Dongguan Mosque, and experience the beauty of plateau religion and nature. Core sights include Qinghai Lake (Erlangjian Scenic Area), Kumbum Monastery, Dongguan Mosque and the Qinghai Tibetan Culture Museum. They cover history, nature and city life and can be combined freely. Pair the core sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or natural areas as time allows.

Food Culture: Xining's food blends the specialities of many ethnic groups: hand-grabbed mutton is true to its original flavour, niangpi is tangy and appetising, ga mianpian is warming and good value, and tianpei is sweet-sour and cooling — halal food is plentiful.

Qinghai Lake (Erlangjian Scenic Area)

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Qinghai Lake is China's largest inland saltwater lake, covering about 4,543 square kilometres at an elevation of 3,196 m. Every July–August the rapeseed flowers bloom along the shore, gold against the blue water, a magnificent sight. Highlights include the Erlangjian Peninsula, Bird Island (April–June), the lakeside rapeseed fields, and sunrise and sunset. Tip: cycling or driving around the lake is recommended; allow 1–2 days. Practical info: located at the Erlangjian Scenic Area, Qinghai Lake, Gonghe County, Hainan Prefecture, Qinghai Province; ticket ¥90; opening hours 08:30–18:00. Kumbum Monastery

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

Kumbum Monastery is one of the six great monasteries of the Gelug school of Tibetan Buddhism. It lies in the Lotus Valley at Lushar Town, Huangzhong District, about 25 km from central Xining. Its history begins with the Lotus-Gathering Stupa built in 1379, the twelfth year of the Ming Hongwu reign, on the birthplace of Tsongkhapa; from 1560, the thirty-ninth year of the Jiajing reign, halls were built around the stupa and the complex gradually expanded into its present form. Butter sculptures, embroidered thangkas and murals are called the monastery's three artistic treasures, and the Butter Sculpture Lantern Festival on the fifteenth day of the first lunar month is one of its grandest ceremonies. Highlights include the Butter Sculpture Hall, the Great Golden Tile Hall, the Small Golden Tile Hall and the Eight Tathagata Stupas. Tip: visit in the morning for better light. Practical info: located at 56 Jinta Road, Huangzhong District, Xining, Qinghai Province; ticket ¥70; opening hours 08:00–17:00. Best season: summer (Jun–Aug) is cool and pleasant, a summer retreat; autumn (Sep–Oct) is colourful.

Tip: Visit in the morning for better light.

  • Address: 56 Jinta Road, Huangzhong District, Xining, Qinghai Province
  • Ticket: ¥70
  • Opening: 08:00–17:00

Dongguan Mosque

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

Dongguan Mosque was first built in the Ming Hongwu period and is one of the largest mosques in north-west China. Its architecture blends Chinese and Islamic styles, and the prayer hall is grand, able to hold tens of thousands of worshippers at once. Highlights include the main prayer hall, the awakening tower and the stele pavilion. Tip: non-Muslims may visit outside prayer times and should dress modestly. Practical info: located at 31 Dongguan Street, Chengdong District, Xining, Qinghai Province; free admission; opening hours 08:00–18:00. Best season: summer (Jun–Aug) is cool and pleasant, a summer retreat; autumn (Sep–Oct) is colourful. Pair with Xining's Qinghai Lake (Erlangjian Scenic Area), Kumbum Monastery and Qinghai Provincial Museum for a half- or full-day itinerary. Check the day's opening and reservation policy before setting out, and allow enough time. Plan your stay according to opening hours and your energy. It can be combined with Qinghai Lake (Erlangjian Scenic Area), Kumbum Monastery, Qinghai Tibetan Culture Museum in Xining for a half- or full-day itinerary.

Tip: Non-Muslims may visit outside prayer times and should dress modestly.
