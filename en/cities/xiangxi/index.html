City Intro

History & Culture: Xiangxi Prefecture lies in northwestern Hunan and is home to Tujia and Miao communities; Fenghuang Ancient Town, Furong Town and Aizhai Bridge together make a destination where mountain scenery and ethnic culture coexist. Xiangxi is where Tujia and Miao cultures meet: hand-waving dances, Miao silverwork, and the literature and art of Shen Congwen and Huang Yongyu together form the cultural imagination of “mysterious Xiangxi”. The Xiangxi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture lies in Hunan Province and has formed a distinct urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, city walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods. Today, museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's historical thread legible.

Geography: The Wuling Mountains run in every direction, and the Youshui and Tuo rivers have cut gorges and river bends; Fenghuang, Furong Town and Dehang represent, respectively, the ancient town, the waterfall town and the Miao village gorge. The Xiangxi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture lies in Hunan Province, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. April–October is best; Fenghuang and Furong Town are most comfortable in spring and autumn; summers are rainy, so watch your footing in the gorges. Natural and cultural landscapes within the prefecture are fairly concentrated, making one- or two-day itineraries by area practical. Light, vegetation and water levels change the look of the landscape with the seasons; check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out. Transport within the prefecture is mainly by road and rail, and the main sights can be linked from the city or county towns.

Highlights: Wander Fenghuang Ancient Town, stay in Furong Town to see the waterfall, walk across Aizhai Bridge, and enter Dehang Miao Village for songs and dances. Core sights include Fenghuang Ancient Town, Furong Town, Aizhai Bridge and Dehang Miao Village. These sights cover different facets such as history, nature and city life, and can be combined freely according to interest. It is worth linking the core sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets into a half-day or full-day itinerary. If time allows, slow the pace and leave room for walking and rest for a fuller experience. First-time visitors can start with the most representative core sights, then add museums, old streets or natural scenery as time allows. Local food is part of the itinerary too, so save mealtimes for long-established restaurants or local markets.

Food Culture: Blood-rice duck, Xiangxi shefan, ginger candy and Xiangxi cured meat: sour-spicy and smoky flavours are the foundation of Xiangxi taste.

Fenghuang Ancient Town

★★★★★Recommend 4.6 / 5

Fenghuang Ancient Town lies in the Xiangxi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture and became famous worldwide through Shen Congwen's novel Border Town. The ancient town nestles against the mountains and the water, with the Tuo River running through it, rows of stilt houses arranged in pleasing disorder on both banks, and bluestone-slab lanes crisscrossing the town. The Rainbow Bridge spans the Tuo River, the Ten Thousand Names Pagoda stands on the riverbank, and the city gate towers and old forts are well preserved. Miao and Tujia customs are strong here, and the silverwork, Miao embroidery and batik crafts are exquisite, making it an outstanding place to experience Xiangxi culture. A National Historical and Cultural City, with the Tuo River running through it and stilt houses, the Rainbow Bridge and the Ten Thousand Names Pagoda dotted along its banks. A night cruise on the Tuo River to enjoy the lights and a stroll along the stone-slab old streets show the ink-wash charm of Xiangxi to the full. Highlights include the Tuo River night scene, the Rainbow Bridge Wind-and-Rain Tower and boating on the Tuo River. Tip: it is best to arrive towards evening, when the Tuo River night scene is most enchanting as the lights come on. Practical info: located at Tuojiang Town, Fenghuang County, Xiangxi Prefecture, Hunan; the ticket is free entry to the ancient town (combo tickets for sights inside the town and Tuo River cruise tickets are purchased separately, with prices subject to the scenic area's notices on the day); opening hours are all day.

Furong Town

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Furong Town was originally called Wangcun and was renamed after the film Hibiscus Town. The old town was built along the Youshui River and the waterfall, with Tujia stilt houses rising in tiers and the waterfall running through the middle of the town; it is one of the most picturesque waterside old towns in Xiangxi. A thousand-year-old town hanging over a waterfall, with Tujia stilt houses built along the Youshui River; the film Hibiscus Town made it famous. Highlights include the waterfall running through the town and the film locations. Tip: combining it with Fenghuang Ancient Town in one day is rushed, so staying one night is recommended; in the rainy season the waterfall carries a large volume of water. Practical info: located at Furong Town, Yongshun County, Xiangxi Prefecture, Hunan; the ticket is about ¥100 (subject to the scenic area's notices); opening hours are 08:00–18:00. Best season: April–October is best; Fenghuang and Furong Town are most comfortable in spring and autumn; summers are rainy, so watch your footing in the gorges. It can be combined with Fenghuang Ancient Town, Aizhai Bridge and Dehang Miao Village in the Xiangxi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture for a half-day or one-day itinerary. It is advisable to confirm the day's opening and reservation policy before setting out and to allow sufficient visiting time.

Aizhai Bridge

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Aizhai Bridge is in Aizhai Town, Jishou City, spanning the Dehang Grand Canyon, with a main span of 1,176 metres and a deck about 355 metres above the valley floor; it opened to traffic in 2012. Together with the Aizhai switchback road below and the Miao villages in the canyon, the bridge forms a “bridge–road–gorge” landscape, and the viewing platform overlooks the Dehang canyon and the spectacle of the Aizhai road. A steel truss suspension bridge spanning the Dehang Grand Canyon, with the deck about 355 metres above the valley floor; it was once among the highest bridges in the world, and the viewing platform offers views of the canyon and the switchback road. Tip: combine it with Dehang Miao Village on the same day; it is windy on the bridge, so those afraid of heights should judge their own limits. Practical info: located at Aizhai Town, Jishou City, Xiangxi Prefecture, Hunan; the ticket is about ¥100 (includes the sightseeing bus, subject to the scenic area's notices); opening hours are 08:30–17:30. Best season: April–October is best; Fenghuang and Furong Town are most comfortable in spring and autumn; summers are rainy, so watch your footing in the gorges.
