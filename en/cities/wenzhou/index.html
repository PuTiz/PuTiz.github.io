City Intro

History & Culture: Wenzhou was founded in 323 AD and prospered in trade because it 'controls the mountains and is belted by the sea'. In the Southern Song the Yongjia School advocated the study of practical achievement and had a far-reaching influence. After reform and opening up the private economy flourished, the 'Wenzhou Model' became famous nationwide, and Wenzhou people's footprints spread across the world. Wenzhou's culture is distinctive and diverse. Ou-Yue culture has a long history, and Southern Opera, called 'the ancestor of Chinese opera', is still sung around Wenzhou. The Yongjia School's emphasis on practical achievement shaped the commercial spirit of Wenzhou people. Lanjie Fu is Wenzhou's grandest folk temple fair, drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors each year. The entrepreneurial spirit of 'daring to be first in the world' made the 'Wenzhou Model' an emblem of reform and opening up.

Geography: On the southeastern coast of Zhejiang, facing the East China Sea to the east and bordering Fujian to the south, Wenzhou has rolling mountains and meeting rivers and sea, and has long been praised as 'the finest landscape in the southeast'. It has a warm, humid subtropical monsoon climate. Wenzhou lies in Zhejiang Province, where landforms, rivers and climate have shaped the city's layout. Spring (Mar–May) brings flowers across Yandang Mountain; autumn (Sep–Nov) is clear and crisp, with pleasant autumn colour along the Nanxi River. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary can be arranged by area. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons; check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out. Natural and cultural sights around the city can usually be linked by road or rail for a half- or full-day trip.

Highlights: Climb Yandang Mountain for its strange peaks and flying waterfalls, visit the old villages along the Nanxi River, stroll Jiangxin Island for its poetry, and taste seafood and fish cakes — experience the Wenzhou spirit of doing business around the world. Core sights include Yandang Mountain, the Nanxi River, Jiangxin Island and the Nanji Islands. They cover history, nature and city life and can be combined freely. Pair the core sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or natural areas as time allows. Local food is part of the itinerary — save a meal for a time-honoured shop or a local market.

Food Culture: Wenzhou cuisine is based on seafood and values freshness, tenderness and lightness. Fish cakes are springy and savoury, sticky rice is savoury and soft, lantern cakes are crisp outside and tender inside, and duck tongues are rich with soy sauce — all are Wenzhou specialities.

Yandang Mountain

★★★★★Recommend 4.6 / 5

Yandang Mountain lies in Yueqing City. Its name comes from 'a lake on the ridge, thick with reeds, where wild geese roost in autumn'. It is a UNESCO Global Geopark and a national 5A scenic area. The scenic area is known for strange peaks and rocks, ancient caves and stone chambers, and flying waterfalls and streams. Lingfeng's Hand-in-Hand Peak looks like hands pressed together in prayer by day and like a couple leaning together at night; Dalongqiu Waterfall drops 190 m, one of the highest single-drop waterfalls in China. Highlights include the Lingfeng night view, the Lingyan flying traverse and the Fangdong plank walk. Tip: the Lingfeng night view is Yandang's most distinctive experience; staying near the scenic area in the evening is recommended. Practical info: located in Yandang Town, Yueqing City, Wenzhou, Zhejiang Province; tickets Lingfeng day ¥45, Dalongqiu ¥50; opening hours 06:00–18:00. Best season: spring (Mar–May) brings flowers across Yandang Mountain; autumn (Sep–Nov) is clear and crisp, with pleasant autumn colour along the Nanxi River.

Nanxi River

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

The Nanxi River is a national scenic area known for 'beautiful water, strange rocks, many waterfalls and ancient villages'. The water is clear to the bottom, the beaches and woods on both banks are picturesque, and it is the birthplace of Chinese landscape poetry. Many old villages from the Song, Yuan, Ming and Qing survive in the area; Furong, Cangpo and Lishui Street show the simple rural scene. Bamboo-raft drifting is the most relaxing way to experience the Nanxi River. Highlights include Shiwei Rock. Tip: the sights of the Nanxi River are scattered; self-drive or a chartered car is recommended. Practical info: located in Yongjia County, Wenzhou, Zhejiang Province; free admission (some old villages charge); open all day. Best season: spring (Mar–May) brings flowers across Yandang Mountain; autumn (Sep–Nov) is clear and crisp, with pleasant autumn colour along the Nanxi River. Pair with Wenzhou's Yandang Mountain, Jiangxin Island and Nanji Islands for a half- or full-day itinerary. Jiangxin Island

★★★★☆Recommend 4.4 / 5

Jiangxin Island is a small island in the Ou River, famous for Xie Lingyun's lines 'spring grass grows in the pond, garden willows change their birds' and known as the 'Island of Poetry'. The island has the East and West Pagodas, Jiangxin Temple and the Xie Lingyun Memorial Hall. The East Pagoda was built in the Tang and the West Pagoda in the Song; they are landmarks of Wenzhou. Ancient trees tower on the island and the setting is quiet. Highlights include the night view of the Ou River. Tip: go in the evening to enjoy the night view on both banks of the Ou. Practical info: located in the middle reaches of the Ou River, Lucheng District, Wenzhou, Zhejiang Province; ticket ¥30 (including ferry); opening hours 08:00–17:30. Best season: spring (Mar–May) brings flowers across Yandang Mountain; autumn (Sep–Nov) is clear and crisp, with pleasant autumn colour along the Nanxi River. Pair with Wenzhou's Yandang Mountain, Nanxi River and Nanji Islands for a half- or full-day itinerary. It can be combined with Yandang Mountain, Nanxi River, Nanji Islands in Wenzhou for a half- or full-day itinerary.

Tip: Go in the evening to enjoy the night view on both banks of the Ou River.
