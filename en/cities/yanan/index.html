City Intro

History & Culture: Yan'an lies in the heart of the Loess Plateau in northern Shaanxi and is a sacred site of the Chinese revolution. From 1935 to 1948 the CPC Central Committee was based here for thirteen years; Baota Mountain, Zaoyuan and Yangjialing preserve countless revolutionary memories, and the Yan'an spirit was born here. Yan'an is a sacred revolutionary site, and the Yan'an spirit — self-reliance and hard struggle — has had a profound influence. Ansai waist drums are powerful, xintianyou folk songs are high and melodious, and northern Shaanxi paper-cutting is fine and delicate. The public sacrifice at the Yellow Emperor's Mausoleum is a grand ceremony of ancestor worship for the Chinese nation. Yan'an lies in Shaanxi Province and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, city walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods.

Geography: It lies in the heart of the Loess Plateau on the banks of the Yan River, with ravines and gullies and distinctive cave dwellings. The climate is warm-temperate, semi-arid and continental monsoon, with four distinct seasons and abundant sunshine. Yan'an is located in Shaanxi Province, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's layout. Autumn (Sep–Oct) is clear and brisk, with the fragrance of ripening jujubes; spring (Apr–May) brings blossom across the Loess Plateau. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so one- or two-day itineraries by area work well. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons, so check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out. Road and rail are the main connections, and major sights can be linked from the city or county seat.

Highlights: Baota Mountain, the symbol of the revolution; the Yellow River wonder of Hukou Waterfall; and cave-dwelling life, in pursuit of revolutionary origins and northern Shaanxi culture. Core sights include Baota Mountain, the Zaoyuan Revolutionary Site, Hukou Waterfall (Shaanxi side) and the Yan'an Revolutionary Memorial Hall. They cover history, nature and city life and can be combined freely. Pair the core sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or natural areas as time permits. Local food is part of the itinerary — save a meal for a time-honoured shop or a local market.

Food Culture: Yan'an's food has a northern Shaanxi character: oil cakes are sweet, fragrant and softly chewy, mutton noodles have a rich broth and tender meat, and potato caca is savoury and soft — full Loess Plateau flavour.

Baota Mountain

Baota Mountain

★★★★★Recommend 4.6 / 5

Baota Mountain is Yan'an's landmark. The ancient pagoda on the hill was first built in the Tang dynasty and rebuilt in the Ming; an octagonal, nine-storey brick tower 44 metres high, it gives a view over the whole city from the top, with the river like a ribbon and the loess hills and gullies spread below. The hill also holds the Zhaixing Tower, beacon towers and the Fan Gong Well, and revolutionary sites including the Yan'an Revolutionary Memorial Hall stand at its foot; after dark the illuminated pagoda is the best spot for photographing the city panorama. Highlights include the pagoda, the Yan'an Revolutionary Memorial Hall, the Zhaixing Tower and the beacon towers. Tip: go up in the evening to enjoy both sunset and the night view. Practical info: located on Baota Mountain Road, Baota District, Yan'an, Shaanxi Province; ticket ¥60; opening hours 08:00–18:00. Best season: autumn (Sep–Oct) is clear and brisk, with the fragrance of ripening jujubes; spring (Apr–May) brings blossom across the Loess Plateau. Combine with the Zaoyuan Revolutionary Site, Hukou Waterfall (Shaanxi side) and the Yan'an Revolutionary Memorial Hall for a half- or full-day itinerary.

Tip: Go up in the evening to enjoy both sunset and the night view.

  • Address: Baota Mountain Road, Baota District, Yan'an, Shaanxi Province
  • Ticket: ¥60
  • Opening: 08:00–18:00

Zaoyuan Revolutionary Site

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Zaoyuan was the seat of the Secretariat of the CPC Central Committee. Mao Zedong, Zhou Enlai, Liu Shaoqi, Zhu De and other leaders lived and worked here, and cave dwellings, the auditorium and other revolutionary sites are preserved in the grounds. Highlights include Mao Zedong's former residence, Zhou Enlai's former residence, the auditorium of the Central Secretariat and the Xingfu Canal. Tip: book in advance and bring your ID card. Practical info: located on Zaoyuan Road, Baota District, Yan'an, Shaanxi Province; admission free (reservation required); opening hours 08:00–17:30. Best season: autumn (Sep–Oct) is clear and brisk, with the fragrance of ripening jujubes; spring (Apr–May) brings blossom across the Loess Plateau. Combine with Baota Mountain, Hukou Waterfall (Shaanxi side) and the Yan'an Revolutionary Memorial Hall for a half- or full-day itinerary. Check the day's opening and booking policy before you go, and allow enough time.

Tip: Book in advance and bring your ID card.

  • Address: Zaoyuan Road, Baota District, Yan'an, Shaanxi Province
  • Ticket: Free (reservation required)
  • Opening: 08:00–17:30

Hukou Waterfall (Shaanxi side)

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Hukou Waterfall is the largest waterfall on the Yellow River and the second largest in China. The river is constricted here into a channel 50 metres wide before plunging into a deep groove to form a great group of falls of immense power. Highlights include the main waterfall, the Ten-Li Dragon Groove, the 'smoke from the riverbed' and the rainbow crossing. Tip: water volume is greatest and the scenery most spectacular in spring (April–May) and autumn (September–October). Practical info: located at Hukou Town, Yichuan County, Yan'an, Shaanxi Province; ticket ¥100; opening hours 07:30–18:30. Best season: autumn (Sep–Oct) is clear and brisk, with the fragrance of ripening jujubes; spring (Apr–May) brings blossom across the Loess Plateau. Combine with Baota Mountain, the Zaoyuan Revolutionary Site and the Yan'an Revolutionary Memorial Hall for a half- or full-day itinerary. Check the day's opening and booking policy before you go, and allow enough time.
