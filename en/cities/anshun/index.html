City Intro

History & Culture: Anshun lies in Guizhou and is the gateway to Huangguoshu Waterfall and other destinations, and was historically a regional transport and trading node. Anshun was established as a garrison in the Ming dynasty and became a military and trading town on the Yunnan-Guizhou road, giving rise to its Tunbao villages. Anshun lies in Guizhou and has formed a distinct urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, walls, lanes, temples and guild halls remain as historical layers from different periods. Museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's history legible today. History is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of everyday life.

Geography: Anshun's core landscapes include Huangguoshu Waterfall, Longgong, Tianlong Tunpu and Getu River, with natural and cultural sights existing side by side within short distances. Anshun lies in Guizhou, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's layout. Best April–October; the waterfall carries the most water in summer. Roads and railways are the main connections, and the major sights can be linked from the city or the county town.

Huangguoshu Waterfall

★★★★★Recommend 4.9 / 5

One of Asia's largest waterfalls, 77.8 metres high and 101 metres wide, viewable from six directions — above, below, left, right, front and back — and you can walk through the Water Curtain Cave. Main highlights include the main waterfall, Doupotang and Tianxingqiao. Visiting tip: water volume is greatest in summer and you will get wet in the Water Curtain Cave, so bring a raincoat; it is on the same day as Tianxingqiao, so wear non-slip shoes. Practical info: the site is at Zhenning Bouyei and Miao Autonomous County, Anshun, Guizhou; ticket ¥160 (includes the main waterfall, Doupotang and Tianxingqiao; shuttle bus extra); opening hours 07:00–18:00. Best season: April–October; the waterfall carries the most water in summer. Bring rain gear in the rainy season, as the scenic-area steps are slippery. It can be combined with Longgong, Tianlong Tunpu and Getu River in Anshun for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Before setting out, check the day's opening and reservation policy and allow plenty of time. In peak season or on holidays, book ahead and travel off-peak, and allow time for queues and security checks. Plan your stay around the opening hours and your energy.

Longgong Cave

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

One of China's longest water caves; a boat carries visitors through the underground river, where stalactites and waterfalls connect inside the cave, and it is cool in summer. Main highlights include the underground river boat trip, Longmen Waterfall and the Heavenly Pool. Visiting tip: the cave is slippery and cool, so bring a jacket; the boat trip queue is long, so go in the morning. Practical info: the site is at Longgong Town, Xixiu District, Anshun, Guizhou; ticket ¥130 (includes boat ticket; subject to official notice); opening hours 08:30–17:30. Tianlong Tunpu

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

A stone village of descendants of Ming dynasty garrison soldiers, where Dixi opera, Fengyang Han-style dress and stone architecture have been preserved for six hundred years; it is the representative of Anshun's Tunpu culture. Visiting tip: Dixi performances have fixed times, so ask about the schedule in advance; it can be visited on the way on the same day as Huangguoshu. Practical info: the site is at Tianlong Town, Pingba District, Anshun, Guizhou; ticket ¥60 (subject to official notice); opening hours 08:30–18:00. Best season: April–October; the waterfall carries the most water in summer. Bring rain gear in the rainy season, as the scenic-area steps are slippery. Culture

Anshun is a meeting place of Tunbao, Bouyei and Miao cultures. Tunbao communities preserve Ming-style Fengyang dress and Dixi opera, while Bouyei batik, Miao silverwork and the Confucian rites at Anshun's Confucian Temple continue today.
