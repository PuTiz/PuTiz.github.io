City Intro

History & Culture: Yichang lies in Hubei and is the gateway to the Three Gorges Dam and other destinations, and was historically a regional transport and trading node. Yichang, formerly Yiling, was the gateway to the Three Gorges; after opening as a treaty port it became a transshipment harbour for Sichuan-Hubei goods, and the Gezhouba and Three Gorges projects reshaped the city. Yichang lies in Hubei and has formed a distinct urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, walls, lanes, temples and guild halls remain as historical layers from different periods. Museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's history legible today. History is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of everyday life.

Geography: Yichang's core landscapes include the Three Gorges Dam, Three Gorges Folk Village, Qingjiang Gallery and Qu Yuan's Hometown, with natural and cultural sights existing side by side within short distances. Yichang lies in Hubei, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's layout. Best March–May and September–November; the Three Gorges are hot and humid in summer, and the river wind is cold in winter. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary can be arranged by area. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons, so check the weather and official notices before setting out. Roads and railways are the main connections, and the major sights can be linked from the city or the county town.

Highlights: In Yichang, focus on the Three Gorges Dam, Three Gorges Folk Village and Qingjiang Gallery, then leave time for local snacks. Three Gorges Dam

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

The world's largest water conservancy project; Tanziling, the 185 viewing platform and the river-closure memorial park offer panoramic views of the dam and the five-stage ship locks. Visiting tip: a real-name reservation is required, so bring your ID card; the shuttle bus links the various points, and the visit takes at least 3 hours. Practical info: the site is at Sandouping Town, Yiling District, Yichang, Hubei; ticket free (shuttle bus ¥35; reservation required); opening hours 08:00–17:00. Best season: March–May and September–November; the Three Gorges are hot and humid in summer, and the river wind is cold in winter. It can be combined with Three Gorges Folk Village, Qingjiang Gallery and Qu Yuan's Hometown in Yichang for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Three Gorges Folk Village

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

A Tujia culture scenic area beside the Xiling Gorge, where stilted houses, stream-side homes and boatmen's work songs recreate the old life of the Three Gorges. Main highlights include Longjin Stream and Bawang Fort. Visiting tip: the scenic area requires a boat, so check the boat schedule first; combining it with the Three Gorges Dam on the same day is rushed, so it is better to split them. Practical info: the site is at Sandouping Town, Yiling District, Yichang, Hubei; ticket ¥180 (includes boat ticket; subject to official notice); opening hours 08:00–17:30. Culture

Yichang is a meeting place of Three Gorges and Chu cultures. The Dragon Boat Festival customs of Qu Yuan's hometown, dragon-boat racing, Tujia mountain songs and memories of the Three Gorges boat trackers tell the human story of the river.
