Shapotou

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Where the Tengger Desert meets the Yellow River, with sand sledding, sheepskin rafts, dune surfing and a Yellow River zipline all here. Tip: Activities are ticketed separately — check the package tickets first; protect against sun and sand. Practical info: located at Shapotou District, Zhongwei, Ningxia; ticket ¥100 (activities extra); opening hours 08:00–18:00. Best season: April–October; the desert has a big day-night temperature difference, so take sun protection in summer. It can be combined with the Tengger Desert, the Yellow River Homestay Cluster and Gaomiao Temple in Zhongwei for a half-day or one-day itinerary.   • Ticket: ¥100 (activities extra)
  • Opening: 08:00–18:00

Tengger Desert

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

China's fourth-largest desert, with rolling dunes suited to off-roading, camping and stargazing. Highlights include desert off-roading, stargazing camp, sunrise and sunset. Tip: Enter the desert with a licensed camp and do not go deep in alone; carry plenty of water. Practical info: located at Zhongwei, Ningxia; ticket free (desert activities extra); opening hours all day. Best season: April–October; the desert has a big day-night temperature difference, so take sun protection in summer. Yellow River Homestay Cluster

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

A cluster of guesthouses converted from an old village beside the Yellow River, where earth walls, orchards and a river bend form a new model for a northwest holiday. Highlights include the Yellow River bend, earth-walled guesthouses, orchards and sunset. Tip: Book accommodation in advance; non-guests can also walk the public areas. Gaomiao Temple

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

An ancient complex combining Confucianism, Buddhism and Daoism, dense with brick carvings and painted sculptures of the underworld — the landmark of Zhongwei's old town. Highlights include the three teachings in one, brick carvings, underworld scenes and the ancient complex. Tip: Photography is forbidden inside the halls; it is on the way on the same day as Shapotou. Practical info: located at Shapotou District, Zhongwei, Ningxia; ticket ¥30 (subject to official notices); opening hours 08:00–17:30.
