City Intro

History & Culture: Wuhan lies in the eastern Jianghan Plain at the confluence of the Yangtze and Han Rivers and consists of the three towns of Wuchang, Hankou and Hanyang; it has long been known as the 'thoroughfare of nine provinces'. The Panlongcheng site attests to 3,500 years of city history, and in modern times the Wuchang Uprising made Wuhan the birthplace of the Xinhai Revolution. Wuhan is a city where the bold spirit of rivers and lakes meets everyday street life. The revolutionary spirit of the Xinhai Revolution began here, and the Wuchang Uprising fired the first shot that overthrew the feudal monarchy. Han opera and Chu opera carry the distant flavour of Chu culture, and the dockland trading culture witnessed the prosperity of the 'thoroughfare of nine provinces'. Annual events such as the East Lake Cherry Blossom Festival and the Wuhan International Yangtze River Crossing Festival give the city a distinctive charm between historical depth and modern vitality.

Geography: At the confluence of the Yangtze and Han Rivers, the city consists of Wuchang, Hankou and Hanyang. It has a subtropical monsoon climate with four distinct seasons, abundant rain, and a dense network of rivers and lakes. Wuhan lies in Hubei Province, where landforms, rivers and climate have shaped the city's layout. Spring (Mar–Apr) brings cherry blossoms; autumn (Sep–Nov) is clear and crisp and the most pleasant. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary can be arranged by area. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons; check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out. Roads and railways are the main connections, and major sights can be linked from the city or county town.

Highlights: Climb Yellow Crane Tower, enjoy the East Lake cherry blossoms, stroll Hubu Alley and taste hot dry noodles — experience the meeting of river-town boldness and everyday street life. Core sights include Yellow Crane Tower, East Lake Eco-Tourism Scenic Area, Wuhan University and Qingchuan Pavilion. They cover history, nature and city life and can be combined freely. Pair the core sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or natural areas as time allows. Local food is part of the itinerary — save a meal for a time-honoured shop or a local market.

Food Culture: Wuhan's food is famous for its breakfast culture: hot dry noodles with rich sesame paste, doupi crisp outside and soft inside, duck necks numbing and spicy, and hutang fen with a thick fish broth — its name as the 'breakfast capital' is well deserved.

Yellow Crane Tower

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Yellow Crane Tower is one of the three great towers of Jiangnan. It was first built in 223, the second year of the Huangwu era of Eastern Wu in the Three Kingdoms period, and became famous through the Tang poet Cui Hao's line 'The ancients have ridden the yellow crane away; here only the Yellow Crane Tower remains.' The tower is 51.4 m high with five storeys, a pyramidal roof and upturned eaves at every level, grand in scale. From the top, the Yangtze River Bridge spans the river and the three towns of Wuhan lie below. Highlights include the White Cloud Pavilion, the Millennium Auspicious Bell, the main tower and the surrounding gardens. Tip: go in the late afternoon to enjoy both sunset and the night view. Practical info: located at No. 1 Sheshan Xishanpo, Wuchang District, Wuhan, Hubei Province; ticket ¥70; opening hours 08:30–18:00. Best season: spring (Mar–Apr) brings cherry blossoms; autumn (Sep–Nov) is clear and crisp and the most pleasant.

Tip: Go in the late afternoon to enjoy both sunset and the night view.

East Lake Eco-Tourism Scenic Area

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

East Lake is one of China's largest urban lakes, with 33 square kilometres of water, more than five times the size of Hangzhou's West Lake. The scenic area has five sections — Tingtao, Moshan, Luoyan, Chuidi and Baima — and the Moshan Cherry Blossom Garden ranks with Wuhan University's cherry blossoms, drawing many visitors every spring. The East Lake Greenway links the sights and is good for cycling or walking. Highlights include the Tingtao and Luoyan areas. Tip: the cherry blossom season is very busy; visit off-peak. Practical info: located at 16 Yanhu Avenue, Wuchang District, Wuhan, Hubei Province; free admission (some sights charge); opening hours 07:00–19:00. Best season: spring (Mar–Apr) brings cherry blossoms; autumn (Sep–Nov) is clear and crisp and the most pleasant. Pair with Wuhan's Yellow Crane Tower, Wuhan University and Hubei Provincial Museum for a half- or full-day itinerary. It can be combined with Yellow Crane Tower, Wuhan University, Qingchuan Pavilion in Wuhan for a half- or full-day itinerary.

Tip: The cherry blossom season is very busy; visit off-peak.

  • Address: 16 Yanhu Avenue, Wuchang District, Wuhan, Hubei Province
  • Ticket: Free (some sights charge)
  • Opening: 07:00–19:00

Wuhan University

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

Wuhan University is one of China's earliest national universities. The campus is built against Luojia Hill, and its blend of Chinese and Western architecture has earned it the name of 'the most beautiful university'. Every March, a hundred cherry trees along Cherry Blossom Road bloom together against the old buildings, making it one of Wuhan's most popular cherry-blossom spots in spring. Highlights include the old library and the East Lake shore. Tip: during cherry blossom season (mid-to-late March) entry requires advance reservation; weekday visits are recommended. Practical info: located at 299 Bayi Road, Wuchang District, Wuhan, Hubei Province; free admission (reservation required in cherry blossom season); open all day. Best season: spring (Mar–Apr) brings cherry blossoms; autumn (Sep–Nov) is clear and crisp and the most pleasant. Pair with Wuhan's Yellow Crane Tower, East Lake Eco-Tourism Scenic Area and Hubei Provincial Museum for a half- or full-day itinerary.
