City Intro

History & Culture: Jiayuguan lies in Gansu and is known for Jiayuguan Fortress and other destinations, and is an important travel node in the region. Jiayuguan was built in 1372, the fifth year of the Ming Hongwu era, as a key pass at the western end of the Ming Great Wall and a military and transport node on the Hexi Corridor. Jiayuguan lies in Gansu and has formed a distinct urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, walls, lanes, temples and guild halls remain as historical layers from different periods. Museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's history legible today. History is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of everyday life.

Geography: Jiayuguan's core landscapes include Jiayuguan Fortress, the Overhanging Great Wall, the First Pier of the Great Wall and Zixuan Winery, with nature and culture existing side by side within short distances. Jiayuguan lies in Gansu, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's layout. Best May–October; the Gobi has a large day-night temperature difference, so take care with sun protection and hydration. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary can be arranged by area. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons, so check the weather and official notices before setting out. Roads and railways are the main connections, and the major sights can be linked from the city or the county town.

Jiayuguan Fortress

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

The western starting point of the Ming Great Wall and the “First Great Pass under Heaven”; the fortress, barbicans and gate towers form a complete defensive system. Main highlights include Guanghua Tower, Rouyuan Tower and the Qilian snow mountains. Visiting tip: buy the combo ticket with the Overhanging Great Wall and the First Pier; the light is good in the morning. Practical info: the site is at Xiongguan District, Jiayuguan, Gansu; ticket ¥110 (combo ticket; subject to official notice); opening hours 08:00–18:00. Best season: May–October; the Gobi has a large day-night temperature difference, so take care with sun protection and hydration. It can be combined with the Overhanging Great Wall, the First Pier of the Great Wall and Zixuan Winery in Jiayuguan for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Before setting out, check the day's opening and reservation policy and allow plenty of time. In peak season or on holidays, book ahead and travel off-peak, and allow time for queues and security checks. Plan your stay around the opening hours and your energy. If you are carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow more time for security checks and walking.

Tip: Buy the combo ticket with the Overhanging Great Wall and the First Pier; the light is good in the morning.

Overhanging Great Wall

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

A Ming Great Wall built on the steep ridge of the Black Mountain, its wall climbing straight up a slope of about 45 degrees and looking from afar as if hanging. Main highlights include the Black Mountain ridge, the steep wall and the Gobi views. Visiting tip: the steps are steep, so wear non-slip shoes; visit on the same day as the fortress. First Pier of the Great Wall

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

The westernmost beacon tower of the Ming Great Wall, standing on the cliff of the Taolai River gorge, with the deeply cut river valley below. Culture

Jiayuguan is where Great Wall and Silk Road cultures meet. The pass fortress, beacon towers, frontier poetry and local roast-meat food culture tell the story of frontier life at the western end of the Hexi Corridor.
