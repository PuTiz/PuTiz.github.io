City Intro

History & Culture: Yantai lies in the northeast of the Shandong Peninsula where the Yellow Sea and the Bohai Sea meet, and is one of China's first coastal open cities and a famous wine city. Its history is long: it was the site of the ancient Dengzhou port, one of the starting points of the Maritime Silk Road, and the hometown of Qi Jiguang, embodying the spirit of resistance to Japanese pirates. Yantai is the birthplace of the legend of the Eight Immortals Crossing the Sea, and its wine culture has deep roots. The Fishermen's Festival (sea sacrifice) is a traditional event for coastal fishermen, and the century-old underground cellar of the Zhangyu Wine Culture Museum bears witness to the modern industrial history of this treaty port. Yantai lies in Shandong Province and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, city walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods.

Geography: Backed by hills and facing the sea, with a mild climate, it has long been called the Fairy Coast. Penglai Pavilion stands on Danya, Changdao rises like a fairy mountain at sea, and vineyards and apple orchards cover the hills. Yantai is located in Shandong Province, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's layout. Summer (Jun–Sep) is for escaping the heat and watching the sea; autumn (Sep–Oct) brings the fragrance of apples and plump, sweet seafood. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so one- or two-day itineraries by area work well. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons, so check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out. Road and rail are the main connections, and major sights can be linked from the city or county seat.

Highlights: The legend of the Eight Immortals Crossing the Sea at Penglai Pavilion, the strange scenery of Crescent Bay on Changdao and the wine culture of Zhangyu; experience the charm of the Fairy Coast. Core sights include Penglai Pavilion, Changdao (Miaodao Islands), the Zhangyu Wine Culture Museum and Yangma Island. They cover history, nature and city life and can be combined freely. Pair the core sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or natural areas as time permits. Local food is part of the itinerary — save a meal for a time-honoured shop or a local market.

Food Culture: Yantai's seafood is abundant: Penglai xiaomian is fresh, fragrant and smooth; haichang laofan (sea worms with rice) has a deep savoury flavour; menzi is crisp outside and tender within; and Zhangyu wine is celebrated at home and abroad.

Penglai Pavilion

Penglai Pavilion

★★★★★Recommend 4.6 / 5

Penglai Pavilion was first built in the sixth year of Jiayou of the Northern Song (1061) and is ranked with the Yellow Crane Tower, Yueyang Tower and Pavilion of Prince Teng as one of China's four great towers. The pavilion sits on the summit of Danya Hill above the sea, looking like a fairyland when cloud and mist wrap it. Highlights include the main pavilion, the Tianhou Palace, Penglai Water City and Tianheng Hill. Tip: in summer there is a chance of seeing a mirage. Practical info: located at 7 Yingbin Road, Penglai District, Yantai, Shandong Province; ticket ¥100; opening hours 07:30–17:30. Best season: summer (Jun–Sep) is for escaping the heat and watching the sea; autumn (Sep–Oct) brings the fragrance of apples and plump, sweet seafood. Combine with Changdao (Miaodao Islands), the Zhangyu Wine Culture Museum and Yangma Island for a half- or full-day itinerary. Check the day's opening and booking policy before you go, and allow enough time.

Tip: In summer there is a chance of seeing a mirage. Go early to avoid the crowds.

  • Address: 7 Yingbin Road, Penglai District, Yantai, Shandong Province
  • Ticket: ¥100
  • Opening: 07:30–17:30

Changdao (Miaodao Islands)

Changdao (Miaodao Islands)

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

China's offshore boundary follows the line from Laotieshan Cape by Dalian through the Miaodao Islands to Penglai Cape in Shandong, separating the Bohai Sea from the Yellow Sea, and Changdao lies exactly on that line; the islands have 56.8 square kilometres of land, 3,541 square kilometres of sea and 187.8 kilometres of coastline. Changdao was formerly Shandong's only island county; in June 2020, with State Council approval, the county was abolished and incorporated into Penglai District of Yantai. The Changdao National Nature Reserve protects migratory birds and their habitat; environmental protection department records give it an area of 5,300 hectares and more than 240 recorded bird species, with spring and autumn the peak seasons for birdwatching. The steep wall of Jiuzhang Cliff and the pebble beach of Crescent Bay were both shaped by long wave erosion; the strait is windy, and passenger boats are often reduced or suspended in strong winds, so confirm the day's sailings before setting out. Highlights include Wangfu Reef. Tip: boats leave from Penglai Port and take about 45 minutes. Practical info: located at Beichangshan Township, Penglai District, Yantai, Shandong Province (boat required); ticket pass ¥150 (boat fare extra); open all day (boat required).

Tip: Boats leave from Penglai Port and take about 45 minutes.

  • Address: Beichangshan Township, Penglai District, Yantai, Shandong Province (boat required)
  • Ticket: Pass ¥150 (boat fare extra)
  • Opening: All day (boat required)

Zhangyu Wine Culture Museum

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

The Zhangyu Wine Culture Museum is China's first wine museum, showing the century-long development of the Zhangyu company and the culture of winemaking. The great underground cellar was built in 1905 and is still in use. Highlights include the century-old great cellar, the wine culture exhibition hall, the tasting area and the Zhangyu Centenary Culture Square. Tip: the ticket includes a tasting, where several Zhangyu wines can be sampled. Practical info: located at 56 Dama Road, Zhifu District, Yantai, Shandong Province; ticket ¥80 (including wine tasting); opening hours 08:00–17:00. Best season: summer (Jun–Sep) is for escaping the heat and watching the sea; autumn (Sep–Oct) brings the fragrance of apples and plump, sweet seafood. Combine with Penglai Pavilion, Changdao (Miaodao Islands) and Yangma Island for a half- or full-day itinerary. Check the day's opening and booking policy before you go, and allow enough time.

Tip: The ticket includes a tasting where several Zhangyu wines can be sampled.

  • Address: 56 Dama Road, Zhifu District, Yantai, Shandong Province
  • Ticket: ¥80 (including wine tasting)
  • Opening: 08:00–17:00
