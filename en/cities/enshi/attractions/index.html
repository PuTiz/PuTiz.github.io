Enshi Grand Canyon

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Karst cliffs and ground-fissure scenery, with the "One Incense Stick" stone pillar at Qixingzhai and the Yunlong Ground Fissure as the core highlights. Main highlights include the cliff plank walk. Touring tip: the full hike takes about 5–6 hours, so wear hiking shoes; the cableway can save energy. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Tunbao Township, Enshi City, Enshi Prefecture, Hubei Province; ticket ¥170 (includes sightseeing bus); opening hours 08:00–17:00. Best travel season: best April–October, with cool canyons in summer; in the rainy season watch for rockfall and slippery paths. It can be combined with Tenglong Cave, Pingshan Canyon and Nü'er City in Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Tenglong Cave

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

One of Asia's largest dry caves, with a cave mouth 74 metres high and an underground river, stalactites and Tujia song-and-dance performances inside. Touring tip: it is cold inside, so bring a jacket; performances have fixed times. Practical information: the scenic area is located in Lichuan City, Enshi Prefecture, Hubei Province; ticket ¥150 (includes the performance, subject to scenic-area notices); opening hours 08:30–17:30. Pingshan Canyon

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

A canyon that became famous for "floating boat" photographs, where the water is clear to the bottom and a boat on it seems to hang in the air. Main highlights include the clear stream and the plank walk. Touring tip: reservation is required and visitor numbers are capped; the water colours are best on sunny days. Practical information: the scenic area is located in Hefeng County, Enshi Prefecture, Hubei Province; ticket ¥128 (includes boat ticket); opening hours 08:00–17:00. Nü'er City

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

A Tujia-style cultural district where stilt houses, hand-waving dance and a snack night market are concentrated, good for an evening stroll. Main highlights include Tujia snacks. Touring tip: go in the evening, watch the performance first and then eat the snacks.
