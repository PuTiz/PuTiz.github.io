City Intro

History & Culture: Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture is located in Hubei Province and is known for destinations such as Enshi Grand Canyon. Enshi was the seat of Shinan Prefecture in the Qing dynasty, a Tusi homeland and trading corridor on the Hubei-Hunan-Sichuan-Guizhou border; it also served as Hubei's wartime provincial capital. Enshi Tujia and Miao Autonomous Prefecture lies in Hubei Province and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, city walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods. Today museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's historical thread legible. The city's history is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of daily life.

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Tenglong Cave

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

One of Asia's largest dry caves, with a cave mouth 74 metres high and an underground river, stalactites and Tujia song-and-dance performances inside. Culture

Enshi is home to Tujia and Miao communities. The Tujia hand-waving dance, Daughter's Festival, Nuo opera and stilted-house building, together with Enshi Yulu tea-making, reflect life in the Wuling Mountains.
