City Intro

History & Culture: Daxing'anling Prefecture is located in Heilongjiang Province and is known for destinations such as Arctic Village. Daxing'anling was long a hunting ground for indigenous peoples, administered under the Heilongjiang General in the Qing dynasty and later developed through forestry and border transport. Daxing'anling Prefecture lies in Heilongjiang Province and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, city walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods. Today museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's historical thread legible. The city's history is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of daily life. In modern times, transport construction, industrial development and urban renewal have added new layers.

Geography: The core landscapes of Daxing'anling Prefecture include Arctic Village, the First Bend of the Longjiang, Nine Bends and Beihong Village. Daxing'anling Prefecture is located in Heilongjiang Province, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. Best June–August for escaping the heat, and December–February for extreme cold and the aurora; in winter temperatures drop below -30°C, so professional cold-weather gear is essential. Natural and cultural landscapes within the area are fairly concentrated, suitable for arranging one- or two-day itineraries by area. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons and alter how the landscape looks, so check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out. Transport within the area is mainly by road and rail, and the main sights can be linked from the city or county town.

Highlights: When visiting Daxing'anling Prefecture, focus on Arctic Village, the First Bend of the Longjiang and Nine Bends. Core sights include Arctic Village, the First Bend of the Longjiang, Nine Bends and Beihong Village. These sights cover different facets such as history, nature and city life, and can be combined freely according to interest. Culture

Daxing'anling is the ancestral home of the Oroqen and other ethnic groups. Oroqen hunting traditions, birch-bark craft, shamanic beliefs and the frontier life of Mohe's Arctic Village together form the cultural memory of the Greater Khingan forests.
