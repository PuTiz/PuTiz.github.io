Arctic Village

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

China's northernmost village, with the Arctic Monument, the northernmost post office and Christmas Village as photo spots, and aurora and extreme cold in winter. Main highlights include the Heilongjiang River. Touring tip: staying warm is the first priority in winter; around the summer solstice there is midnight sun. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Beiji Town, Mohe City, Daxing'anling Prefecture, Heilongjiang Province; ticket ¥68 (subject to scenic-area notices); opening hours 08:00–17:00. Best travel season: best June–August for escaping the heat, and December–February for extreme cold and the aurora; in winter temperatures drop below -30°C, so professional cold-weather gear is essential. It can be combined with the First Bend of the Longjiang, Nine Bends and Beihong Village in Daxing'anling Prefecture for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Before setting out, confirm that day's opening and reservation policies and allow ample time for the visit. If carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow more time for security checks and walking. Best season: Best June–August for escaping the heat, and December–February for extreme cold and the aurora; in winter temperatures drop below -30°C, so professional cold-weather gear is essential.

Tip: Staying warm is the first priority in winter; around the summer solstice there is midnight sun.

  • Address: Beiji Town, Mohe City, Daxing'anling Prefecture, Heilongjiang Province

  • Opening: 08:00–17:00

First Bend of the Longjiang

★★★★★Recommend 4.6 / 5

The Heilongjiang River makes a huge Ω-shaped bend in the forest; climb the viewing platform to see the river bend and the sea of forest. Main highlights include the Ω-shaped river bend and the border river. Touring tip: there are many steps, so wear non-slip shoes; it is a long distance from Arctic Village for the same day. Nine Bends

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

The Emuer River winds through the wetland in nine bends and eighteen turns, and morning mist and sunset make the best photographs. Main highlights include the wetland river bends and the viewing platform. Touring tip: best in the early morning and at dusk; bring mosquito protection. Beihong Village

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5
