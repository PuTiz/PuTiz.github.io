City Intro

History & Culture: Shangrao is located in Jiangxi Province and is known for destinations such as Huangling. Shangrao, formerly Xinzhou, was a transport and trading centre in northeastern Jiangxi; Wuyuan long belonged to Huizhou, creating a distinctive Huizhou cultural enclave. Shangrao lies in Jiangxi Province and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, city walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods. Today museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's historical thread legible. The city's history is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of daily life. In modern times, transport construction, industrial development and urban renewal have added new layers.

Geography: The core landscapes of Shangrao include Huangling, Jiangwan, Likeng and Moon Bay. Shangrao is located in Jiangxi Province, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. In mountain areas pay attention to altitude and changing weather; in river valleys and lake areas pay attention to water levels and the flood season.

Highlights: When visiting Shangrao, focus on Huangling, Jiangwan and Likeng. Core sights include Huangling, Jiangwan, Likeng and Moon Bay. Huangling

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Wuyuan's most famous autumn-drying ancient village, with Huizhou-style houses built along the slope; the drying trays and terraced flower fields are the classic images. Main highlights include the cableway. Touring tip: rapeseed flowers in March–April and autumn drying in September–November; there are cableway queues. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Huangling, Wuyuan County, Shangrao City, Jiangxi Province; ticket ¥145 (includes cableway); opening hours 07:30–17:30. Best travel season: March–April for rapeseed flowers and October–November for autumn colours; Wuyuan is rainy in spring. It can be combined with Jiangwan, Likeng and Moon Bay in Shangrao for a half-day or full-day itinerary. If carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow more time for security checks and walking. Culture

Shangrao is a meeting place of Huizhou and Gan-Po cultures. The Huizhou villages of Wuyuan, Nuo dance, Hui opera, She inkstone making and Wuyuan tea culture sustain the folk traditions of northeastern Jiangxi.
