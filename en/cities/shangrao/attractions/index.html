Huangling

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Wuyuan's most famous autumn-drying ancient village, with Huizhou-style houses built along the slope; the drying trays and terraced flower fields are the classic images. Main highlights include the cableway. Touring tip: rapeseed flowers in March–April and autumn drying in September–November; there are cableway queues. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Huangling, Wuyuan County, Shangrao City, Jiangxi Province; ticket ¥145 (includes cableway); opening hours 07:30–17:30. Best travel season: March–April for rapeseed flowers and October–November for autumn colours; Wuyuan is rainy in spring. It can be combined with Jiangwan, Likeng and Moon Bay in Shangrao for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Jiangwan

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

A representative Huizhou ancient village in Wuyuan, with the Xiaojiang ancestral hall, old houses and lanes well preserved, and fewer visitors than Huangling. Main highlights include Huizhou-style old houses and the countryside. Touring tip: it can be done with Huangling on the same day by chartered car; in spring the flower fields are outside the village. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Jiangwan Town, Wuyuan County, Shangrao City, Jiangxi Province; ticket ¥60 (subject to scenic-area notices); opening hours 07:30–17:30. Likeng

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

A Huizhou village of the small-bridge-over-flowing-water kind, with a stream running through the village and houses built along the water, good for a slow walk and photographs. Main highlights include Huizhou-style houses and the ancestral hall. Touring tip: fairly commercialised, with fewer people in the morning; on the same route as Jiangwan. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Qiukou Town, Wuyuan County, Shangrao City, Jiangxi Province; ticket ¥60 (subject to scenic-area notices); opening hours 07:30–17:30. Moon Bay

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

A classic viewpoint on a Wuyuan river bend; during rapeseed season the water reflects the flower fields and Huizhou villages, good for roadside photographs. Main highlights include the reflections. Touring tip: roadside parking is heavy in flower season, so take care; outside flower season it is fairly ordinary. Practical information: the scenic area is located in Wuyuan County, Shangrao City, Jiangxi Province; ticket Free; opening hours Open all day.
