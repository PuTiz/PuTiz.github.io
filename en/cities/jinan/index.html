City Intro

History & Culture: Jinan, also known as the City of Springs, lies in west-central Shandong on the south bank of the lower Yellow River and is the provincial capital. Its seventy-two famous springs bubble without cease, earning it the name City of Springs; it is one of the birthplaces of Longshan culture, and the ci poets Li Qingzhao and Xin Qiji both came from here. Jinan is a city steeped in spring culture. The seventy-two famous springs flow endlessly, the city moat links the spring pools and gardens, and Qushuiting Street has little bridges over running water, letting visitors feel a leisurely delight in clear springs and stone even in a busy city. Longshan culture originated here, and the ci lyrics of Li Qingzhao and Xin Qiji are sung through the ages. Annual events such as the Jinan International Spring Festival and the Daming Lake Lotus Festival show the City of Springs' distinctive charm between tradition and modernity.

Geography: At the northern foot of Mount Tai and on the bank of the Yellow River, with Mount Qianfo to the south and the Yellow River to the north, Jinan has a warm-temperate, semi-humid monsoon climate with four distinct seasons. The three jets of Baotu Spring leaping up, the vast misty expanse of Daming Lake and the distant chanting of Qianfo Mountain form a picture of 'a city of hills, half a city of lake'. Jinan lies in Shandong, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's layout. Spring (Apr–May) when the springs gush, and autumn (Sep–Oct) with clear, crisp skies, are the most pleasant times to visit. Natural and cultural sites are fairly concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary by area works well. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons; check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out.

Highlights: Watch Baotu Spring gush, see the lotus blooms at Daming Lake, climb Qianfo Mountain for the view and taste bazi pork and youxuan pastry — experience the City of Springs' unique mood of clear water over stone. Core sights include Baotu Spring, Daming Lake, Qianfo Mountain and the Shandong Museum. They cover history, nature and city life, and can be combined freely to suit your interests. Pair the main sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or natural areas as time permits. Local food is part of the trip too — save a meal for a time-honoured shop or a local market.

Food Culture: Jinan's food is plain and hearty: bazi pork is rich but not greasy, youxuan pastry is crisp and fragrant, tianmo is a savoury, warming soup, and spring-water tofu is silky and delicately sweet — the essence of the City of Springs' table.

Baotu Spring

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Baotu Spring is the first of Jinan's seventy-two famous springs and is known as the finest spring under heaven. The pool is rectangular, and three clear jets gush day and night, throwing spray in a spectacular display. Baotu Spring Park also holds other famous springs such as Shuyu Spring and Mapao Spring, as well as the Li Qingzhao Memorial and Wanzhu Garden. In his essay 'Baotu Spring', Lao She wrote: 'In the middle of the pool there are three large springs, rolling day and night without rest.' First of Jinan's seventy-two famous springs, with three clear jets gushing day and night, 'Baotu leaping skyward' is one of the eight old views of Jinan; pavilions and terraces dot the garden, and it is the most delightful spring in Lao She's writing. Highlights include the three springs and Guanlan Pavilion. Tip: the water is clearest in the early morning, so go before noon. Practical info: located at 1 Baotu Spring South Road, Lixia District, Jinan, Shandong; ticket ¥40 (peak season); opening hours 07:00–19:00. Best season: spring (Apr–May) when the springs gush, and autumn (Sep–Oct) with clear, crisp skies, are the most pleasant times to visit. Combine with Daming Lake, Qianfo Mountain and Lingyan Temple in Jinan for a half-day or full-day itinerary.

Tip: The water is clearest in the early morning, so go before noon.

  • Address: 1 Baotu Spring South Road, Lixia District, Jinan, Shandong
  • Ticket: ¥40 (peak season)
  • Opening: 07:00–19:00

Daming Lake

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Daming Lake is one of Jinan's three great sights, formed where many of the city's springs converge, and is famed for 'lotus on four sides, willows on three; a city of hills, half a city of lake'. Blue water ripples, weeping willows line the banks, and lotus blooms in summer make a glorious sight. Chaoran Tower is the lake's landmark, its night illuminations dazzling — a newly popular spot in Jinan. Highlights include Lixia Pavilion, Tie Gong Shrine and the lotus pond. A natural lake fed by many springs, with 'lotus on four sides, willows on three' and the brilliantly lit Chaoran Tower at night, it is the core symbol of Jinan's City of Springs scenery. Tip: most beautiful in summer when the lotus blooms. Practical info: located at 271 Daming Lake Road, Lixia District, Jinan, Shandong; free entry (some sights charge); opening hours 06:30–22:00. Best season: spring (Apr–May) when the springs gush, and autumn (Sep–Oct) with clear, crisp skies, are the most pleasant times to visit. Combine with Baotu Spring, Qianfo Mountain and Lingyan Temple in Jinan for a half-day or full-day itinerary.

Tip: Most beautiful in summer when the lotus blooms.

  • Address: 271 Daming Lake Road, Lixia District, Jinan, Shandong
  • Ticket: Free (some sights charge)
  • Opening: 06:30–22:00

Qianfo Mountain

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

Qianfo Mountain is one of Jinan's three great sights. At 285 metres, it takes its name from its many Buddhist stone carvings. Hundreds of Sui dynasty stone Buddhas are cut into the cliffs, and Xingguo Temple, first built in the Sui and Tang dynasties, still draws worshippers today. From the summit you can look over all of Jinan, with the Yellow River like a ribbon to the north and the City of Springs spread out below. Ancient trees tower on the slopes, making it a fine place for hiking and for Buddhist prayer. One of Jinan's three great sights, with Sui dynasty cliff carvings of Buddhas cut into the rock; from the summit you can look over the whole City of Springs, while incense burns steadily at Xingguo Temple. Highlights include the Sui dynasty stone carvings, Lishan Courtyard and the summit viewing platform. Tip: it is best to climb from the south gate, passing the stone carvings along the way. Practical info: located at 18 Jingshi Road No. 1, Lixia District, Jinan, Shandong; ticket ¥30; opening hours 06:30–18:00. Best season: spring (Apr–May) when the springs gush, and autumn (Sep–Oct) with clear, crisp skies, are the most pleasant times to visit. Combine with Baotu Spring, Daming Lake and Lingyan Temple in Jinan for a half-day or full-day itinerary.

Tip: It is best to climb from the south gate, passing the stone carvings along the way.

  • Address: 18 Jingshi Road No. 1, Lixia District, Jinan, Shandong
