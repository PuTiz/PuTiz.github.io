City Intro

History & Culture: Yanbian Korean Autonomous Prefecture is in Jilin and is known for destinations such as Changbai Mountain's North Slope. Yanbian lies in the Tumen River basin; the Qing established the Hunchun Vice-Dutong, and Korean migrants settled and farmed the area in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, shaping today's ethnic makeup. Yanbian lies in Jilin and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods. Today museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's history legible. The city's history is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of daily life. In modern times, transport building, industrial development and urban renewal have added further layers.

Geography: Yanbian's core landscapes include Changbai Mountain's North Slope, Tianchi Lake, Changbai Waterfall and Erdaobaihe Town. Yanbian lies in Jilin, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. June–September is best for escaping the heat, and December–February for skiing and hot springs; Changbai Mountain is rainy in summer and roads close in winter. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary by area works well. Light, vegetation and water levels change the look of the landscape with the seasons; check the weather and official notices before setting out. Roads and railways are the main connections, and the main sights can be linked from the city or county town.

Changbai Mountain North Slope

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

The most developed climbing route on Changbai Mountain, transferring to eco-buses and shuttle vans straight up to the main peak to see Tianchi and Changbai Waterfall. Highlights include Lvyuan Pool and the Underground Forest. Tip: Whether Tianchi is visible depends on the weather; it is cold on the mountain, so bring a jacket. Practical info: located at Erdaobaihe Town, Antu County, Yanbian, Jilin; ticket ¥105 (sightseeing bus ¥85); opening hours 07:00–17:00. Best season: June–September for escaping the heat, and December–February for skiing and hot springs; Changbai Mountain is rainy in summer and roads close in winter. It can be combined with Tianchi Lake, Changbai Waterfall and Erdaobaihe Town in Yanbian for a half-day or one-day itinerary. Before setting out, confirm the day's opening and reservation policies and allow plenty of time for the visit. In peak season or on holidays, book ahead and travel outside peak times, and allow time for queues and security checks. Plan your length of stay around the opening hours and your energy. If carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow more time for security checks and walking.

  • Address: Erdaobaihe Town, Antu County, Yanbian, Jilin
  • Ticket: ¥105 (sightseeing bus ¥85)
  • Opening: 07:00–17:00

Tianchi Lake

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

China's deepest crater lake, with the water surface at 2,189 metres; on clear days it is brilliantly blue, and cloud and mist arrive extremely fast. Highlights include the lake water, the sea of clouds and the main peak. Tip: Seeing Tianchi takes luck; visibility is better in the morning; do not cross the guardrail. Practical info: located at Antu County, Yanbian, Jilin; ticket included in the North Slope ticket; opening hours 07:00–17:00. Best season: June–September for escaping the heat, and December–February for skiing and hot springs; Changbai Mountain is rainy in summer and roads close in winter. It can be combined with Changbai Mountain's North Slope, Changbai Waterfall and Erdaobaihe Town in Yanbian for a half-day or one-day itinerary. Before setting out, confirm the day's opening and reservation policies and allow plenty of time for the visit. In peak season or on holidays, book ahead and travel outside peak times, and allow time for queues and security checks. Plan your length of stay around the opening hours and your energy. If carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow more time for security checks and walking.

  • Address: Antu County, Yanbian, Jilin
  • Ticket: Included in the North Slope ticket
  • Opening: 07:00–17:00

Changbai Waterfall

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Tianchi's water drops 68 metres through a gap here; it is the true source of the Songhua River and freezes into an ice waterfall in winter. Highlights include the 68-metre waterfall, the source of the Songhua River and the hot springs. Tip: It is on the same route as Tianchi; the walkway freezes in winter. Practical info: located at Antu County, Yanbian, Jilin; ticket included in the North Slope ticket; opening hours 07:00–17:00. Culture

Yanbian is a Korean cultural heartland. Korean long-drum dance, sangmo dance, Arirang songs, cold noodles and kimchi-making, together with festivals in Yanji, shape the culture of the Tumen River borderland.
