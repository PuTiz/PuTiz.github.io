Hanpokou

Hanpokou

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

The best spot on Lushan to watch the sunrise over Poyang Lake; on clear days you can see the lake surface meeting the sea of clouds. Main highlights include Litou Point and the viewing platform. Touring tip: go at dawn and bring a jacket; whether you can see the lake depends on the weather. Practical information: the scenic area is located in the Lushan Scenic Area, Jiujiang City, Jiangxi Province; ticket included in the Lushan main ticket; opening hours 06:00–18:00. Best travel season: best March–May and September–November; Lushan is a summer escape and has rime ice in winter. It can be combined with Sandiequan, Meilu and Huajing in Jiujiang for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Sandiequan

Sandiequan

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Lushan's most spectacular waterfall, with a total drop of 155 metres in three tiers; there is a saying that "without reaching Sandiequan, you are not a Lushan guest". Main highlights include the three-tier waterfall, the 155-metre drop, the cable car and the gorge. Touring tip: there are many steps, and the round trip takes about 2–3 hours; the water flow is strong in the rainy season. Practical information: the scenic area is located in the Lushan Scenic Area, Jiujiang City, Jiangxi Province; ticket ¥64 (includes round-trip cable car, subject to scenic-area notices); opening hours 07:00–17:00. Meilu

Meilu

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

Lushan's most famous villa, the summer capital residence of Soong Mei-ling and Chiang Kai-shek, and also the historical scene of the KMT–CCP negotiations. Main highlights include the villa architecture, historical displays, Soong Mei-ling's bedroom and the Lushan Conference. Touring tip: on the same route as the former site of the Lushan Conference; photography is prohibited indoors. Practical information: the scenic area is located in the Lushan Scenic Area, Jiujiang City, Jiangxi Province; ticket ¥25 (subject to scenic-area notices); opening hours 08:00–17:00. Huajing

Huajing

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

The site of Bai Juyi's line "In the human world, April's fragrances are all gone; peach blossoms in the mountain temple are just beginning to bloom", where spring peach blossoms reflect in Ruqin Lake. Main highlights include Bai Juyi's Thatched Cottage and Huajing Pavilion. Touring tip: most beautiful in spring; on the same route as Jinxiu Valley. Practical information: the scenic area is located in the Lushan Scenic Area, Jiujiang City, Jiangxi Province; ticket included in the Lushan main ticket; opening hours 06:00–18:00.
