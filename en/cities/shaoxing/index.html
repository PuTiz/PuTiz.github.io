City Intro

History & Culture: Shaoxing is in Zhejiang and is known for destinations such as Lu Xun's Hometown; it is an important travel hub in the region. Shaoxing, formerly Yuezhou, was the capital of the State of Yue in the Spring and Autumn period and became a centre of rice-wine brewing, legal clerks and learning along the eastern Zhejiang canal in the Ming and Qing. Shaoxing lies in Zhejiang and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods. Today museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's history legible. The city's history is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of daily life.

Geography: Shaoxing's core landscapes include Lu Xun's Hometown, Shen Garden, Lanting and East Lake, with nature and culture existing side by side within a short distance. Shaoxing lies in Zhejiang, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. March–May and September–November are best; the plum-rain season is wet and the stone-paved lanes are slippery. Light, vegetation and water levels change the look of the landscape with the seasons; check the weather and official notices before setting out. Roads and railways are the main connections, and the main sights can be linked from the city or county town. In mountain areas watch altitude and changing weather; in river valleys and lake areas watch water levels and the flood season.

Culture

Shaoxing is a centre of Yue culture. Lu Xun's literary legacy, Yue and Shao opera, yellow-rice-wine brewing, black-awning boats and village opera, together with the calligraphy tradition of Lanting, define the old water town.
