Xiaolianzhuang

Xiaolianzhuang

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

The private garden of Liu Yong, the richest man in Nanxun; the lotus pond, archways and library combine Jiangnan gardens with merchant culture. Highlights include the Liu family ancestral temple and Dongsheng Pavilion. Tip: The combo ticket covers Xiaolianzhuang and Jiayetang; the lotus is most beautiful in summer. Practical info: located at Nanxun Town, Nanxun District, Huzhou, Zhejiang; ticket ¥100 (Nanxun old-town combo ticket); opening hours 08:00–17:00. Best season: March–May and September–November; Nanxun's water town is even more evocative in the rain. It can be combined with Zhang Shiming Residence, Baijianlou and Jiayetang Library in Huzhou for a half-day or one-day itinerary. Zhang Shiming Residence

Zhang Shiming Residence

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

The residence of the Zhang family, one of Nanxun's “Four Elephants”, where Chinese halls coexist with a French ballroom and stained glass — a classic blend of East and West. Highlights include the brick carvings. Tip: Focus on the details of the East-West blend; entry is with the combo ticket. Practical info: located at Nanxun Town, Nanxun District, Huzhou, Zhejiang; ticket included in the combo ticket; opening hours 08:00–17:00. Baijianlou

Baijianlou

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

A Ming-era residential group built along the river, where more than a hundred houses and arcades form a continuous row — the most lived-in stretch of Nanxun. Highlights include the riverside houses, stone bridges and morning mist. Tip: Go early in the morning for the mist and the people washing clothes; no ticket is needed. Practical info: located at Nanxun Town, Nanxun District, Huzhou, Zhejiang; ticket free; opening hours all day. Best season: March–May and September–November; Nanxun's water town is even more evocative in the rain. It can be combined with Xiaolianzhuang, Zhang Shiming Residence and Jiayetang Library in Huzhou for a half-day or one-day itinerary. Jiayetang Library

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

A private library built by Liu Chenggan, combining garden and book stacks, which once held hundreds of thousands of volumes of ancient books. Highlights include the library, garden, ancient books and steles. Tip: See it together with the Jiayetang garden; entry is with the combo ticket.
