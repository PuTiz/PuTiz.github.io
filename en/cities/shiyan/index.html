City Intro

History & Culture: Shiyan is located in Hubei Province and is known for destinations such as the Wudang Mountain Golden Summit. Shiyan lies in the Qinba Mountains on the upper Han River, formerly under Junzhou and Yunyang prefectures; in modern times the Second Automobile Works turned it into an automotive city. Shiyan lies in Hubei Province and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, city walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods. Today museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's historical thread legible. The city's history is written not only in sites and museums but also in dialect, food, festivals and the details of daily life. In modern times, transport construction, industrial development and urban renewal have added new layers.

Geography: The core landscapes of Shiyan include the Wudang Mountain Golden Summit, Zixiao Palace, Taizipo and Nanyan Palace. Shiyan is located in Hubei Province, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. Wudang Mountain Golden Summit

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

The golden hall on the summit of Tianzhu Peak, the main peak of Wudang Mountain, cast in bronze and gilded in the Ming dynasty; from the top you can see cloud seas and the surrounding mountains. Main highlights include Taihe Palace. Touring tip: to reach the summit you can take the cableway and walk; the Golden Summit charges a separate small ticket. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Wudang Mountain, Danjiangkou City, Shiyan City, Hubei Province; ticket ¥140 (sightseeing bus ¥100; the small Golden Summit ticket is separate); opening hours 07:00–17:30. Best travel season: best April–June and September–October; Wudang Mountain is cool in summer and snowy in winter. It can be combined with Zixiao Palace, Taizipo and Nanyan Palace in Shiyan for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Before setting out, confirm that day's opening and reservation policies and allow ample time for the visit. If carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow more time for security checks and walking. Before setting out, confirm that day's opening and reservation policies so that a temporary closure does not disrupt your itinerary. Best season: Best April–June and September–October; Wudang Mountain is cool in summer and snowy in winter.

Zixiao Palace

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

The most completely preserved Ming-dynasty palace on Wudang Mountain, backed by Zhanqi Peak, with the Longhu Hall, Shifang Hall and Zixiao Hall along the central axis. Main highlights include Ming-dynasty architecture. Touring tip: on the same day as the Golden Summit, visit Zixiao first and then the summit; no photography inside the halls. Taizipo

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

Culture

Shiyan is a meeting place of Wudang and Han River cultures. Wudang Daoist rites, Wudang martial arts, Daoist music, Han River shipping memories and the Qixi customs of Yunxi form the cultural traditions of the Qinba Mountains.
