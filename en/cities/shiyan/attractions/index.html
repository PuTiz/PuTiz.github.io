Wudang Mountain Golden Summit

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

The golden hall on the summit of Tianzhu Peak, the main peak of Wudang Mountain, cast in bronze and gilded in the Ming dynasty; from the top you can see cloud seas and the surrounding mountains. Main highlights include Taihe Palace. Touring tip: to reach the summit you can take the cableway and walk; the Golden Summit charges a separate small ticket. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Wudang Mountain, Danjiangkou City, Shiyan City, Hubei Province; ticket ¥140 (sightseeing bus ¥100; the small Golden Summit ticket is separate); opening hours 07:00–17:30. Best travel season: best April–June and September–October; Wudang Mountain is cool in summer and snowy in winter. It can be combined with Zixiao Palace, Taizipo and Nanyan Palace in Shiyan for a half-day or full-day itinerary. If carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow more time for security checks and walking. Before setting out, confirm that day's opening and reservation policies so that a temporary closure does not disrupt your itinerary. Best season: Best April–June and September–October; Wudang Mountain is cool in summer and snowy in winter.

Zixiao Palace

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

The most completely preserved Ming-dynasty palace on Wudang Mountain, backed by Zhanqi Peak, with the Longhu Hall, Shifang Hall and Zixiao Hall along the central axis. Main highlights include Ming-dynasty architecture. Touring tip: on the same day as the Golden Summit, visit Zixiao first and then the summit; no photography inside the halls. Practical information: the scenic area is located at Wudang Mountain, Danjiangkou City, Shiyan City, Hubei Province; ticket included in the Wudang Mountain main ticket (small tickets separate); opening hours 07:00–17:30. Taizipo

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

A turning point in the Wudang Mountain building complex; the Nine-Bend Yellow River Wall and the One Pillar Twelve Beams are classics, and it is a good place to see how mountain architecture handles a slope. Main highlights include Fuzhen Temple and slope architecture. Touring tip: on the same route as Zixiao Palace; there are many tour groups, so there are fewer people in the morning. Nanyan Palace

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

The most precipitous palace on Wudang Mountain, with the halls set into the cliff and the Dragon-Head Incense facing a deep valley. Main highlights include the cliff halls, South Cliff and Feisheng Cliff. Touring tip: the Dragon-Head Incense area is dangerous, so do not climb over the railing; it is on the same day as Zixiao Palace.
