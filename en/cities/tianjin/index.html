City Intro

History & Culture: Tianjin rose through grain transport on the Grand Canal and the salt trade. A garrison was established and the city walled in the early Ming, and it has long been a north–south thoroughfare where merchants gathered. As a treaty port in modern times it left a great deal of Western architecture, creating a cityscape where East and West, old and new, blend. Tianjin is famous as a hometown of folk performing arts, and xiangsheng crosstalk and kuaiban clapper talk are close to people's hearts. Folk arts such as Yangliuqing New Year paintings and Clay Figure Zhang painted sculpture are renowned nationwide. The villas of the Five Great Avenues and the Italian Style Area carry memories of modern history, while Ancient Culture Street preserves Tianjin's folk customs. The Hai River runs through the city with a captivating night view, and the Tianjin Eye Ferris wheel has become a new landmark. The city offers both the everyday flavours of mahua and baozi and the bold character of dock culture, and it is warm, humorous and full of life.

Geography: Tianjin lies in the northeast of the North China Plain where the five main tributaries of the Hai River meet, facing the Bohai Sea to the east, and has a warm-temperate, semi-humid monsoon climate. As a municipality directly under the central government, Tianjin's urban space, rivers and climate have shaped one another into a compact, multi-centred city. Autumn (September–November) is crisp and clear; spring (April–May) is also pleasant. Natural and cultural sights are fairly concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary by area works well. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons; check the weather and official notices before setting out. Roads and railways are the main connections, and the main sights can be linked from the city or county town. In the mountains watch altitude and changing weather, and around rivers and lakes watch water levels and flood season.

Highlights: Wander Ancient Culture Street for Tianjin folk customs, stroll the Five Great Avenues for architecture from around the world, ride the Tianjin Eye for night views over the city and taste authentic Tianjin snacks. Core sights include Ancient Culture Street, the Five Great Avenues, the Tianjin Eye and Panshan Scenic Area. They cover history, nature and city life and can be combined freely. Pair the main sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or natural areas as time allows.

Food Culture: Tianjin snacks are famous nationwide: Goubuli baozi have thin skins and generous fillings, jianbing guozi are crisp and tasty, Erduoyan zhagao are crisp outside and soft within, and Shibajie mahua are crisp and sweet.

Ancient Culture Street

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

Ancient Culture Street is Tianjin's most distinctive tourist shopping street, centred on the Tianhou Temple and 580 metres long. It gathers famous Tianjin folk arts and crafts such as Yangliuqing New Year paintings, Clay Figure Zhang painted sculpture and Wei's kites. Highlights include the Yangliuqing New Year Painting Museum and the Clay Figure Zhang Art Museum. An old street centred on the Tianhou Temple, it gathers Yangliuqing New Year paintings, Clay Figure Zhang and other time-honoured names, where Tianjin folk customs and street life can be felt. Tip: go towards evening to enjoy both the daytime folk atmosphere and the night lighting. Practical info: the street is on Dongmen Inner Street, Nankai District, Tianjin; admission free; opening hours 09:00–21:00. Best season: autumn (September–November) is crisp and clear; spring (April–May) is also pleasant. Combine with the Five Great Avenues, the Tianjin Eye and Panshan Scenic Area for a half- or full-day route. Confirm the day's opening and reservation policy before you go and allow ample time. In peak season or on holidays, book in advance, travel off-peak and allow time for queues and security checks.

Tip: Go towards evening to enjoy both the daytime folk atmosphere and the night lighting.

  • Address: Dongmen Inner Street, Nankai District, Tianjin
  • Ticket: Free
  • Opening: 09:00–21:00

Five Great Avenues

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

The Five Great Avenues is Tianjin's most distinctive historic and cultural district, preserving more than 2,000 villas built in the 1920s and 1930s in British, French, Italian, German, Spanish and other styles. Highlights include Minyuan Square, Prince Qing's Mansion and Xiannong Courtyard. A historic district enclosed by five roads, it gathers more than 2,000 villas in styles from many countries and is known as an exhibition of world architecture. Tip: a horse-drawn carriage tour is recommended, as the guide explains the history of each building. Practical info: the district is on Chongqing Road and nearby streets, Heping District, Tianjin; admission free (some exhibition venues charge admission); open all day. Best season: autumn (September–November) is crisp and clear; spring (April–May) is also pleasant. Combine with Ancient Culture Street, the Tianjin Eye and Panshan Scenic Area for a half- or full-day route. Confirm the day's opening and reservation policy before you go and allow ample time. In peak season or on holidays, book in advance, travel off-peak and allow time for queues and security checks.

Tip: A horse-drawn carriage tour is recommended, as the guide explains the history of each building.

  • Address: Chongqing Road and nearby streets, Heping District, Tianjin
  • Ticket: Free (some exhibition venues charge admission)
  • Opening: Open all day

Tianjin Eye

★★★★☆Recommend 4.5 / 5

The Tianjin Eye is the only Ferris wheel in the world built on a bridge. It is 120 metres tall with a diameter of 110 metres, has 48 transparent cabins and takes about 30 minutes to complete one revolution. Highlights include the Ferris wheel ride, the Hai River night view and Yongle Bridge. A giant Ferris wheel spanning the Hai River, about 120 metres tall, it offers views over the city at night and is one of Tianjin's modern landmarks. Tip: ride towards evening to enjoy both sunset and the night view. Practical info: the wheel is at Yongle Bridge, Sanchahekou, Hongqiao District, Tianjin; ticket about ¥70; opening hours 09:30–21:30. Best season: autumn (September–November) is crisp and clear; spring (April–May) is also pleasant. Combine with Ancient Culture Street, the Five Great Avenues and Panshan Scenic Area for a half- or full-day route. Confirm the day's opening and reservation policy before you go and allow ample time. In peak season or on holidays, book in advance, travel off-peak and allow time for queues and security checks.

Tip: Ride towards evening to enjoy both sunset and the night view.

  • Address: Yongle Bridge, Sanchahekou, Hongqiao District, Tianjin
  • Ticket: About ¥70
  • Opening: 09:30–21:30
