City Intro

History & Culture: Qingdao lies on the southern coast of the Shandong Peninsula by the Yellow Sea. As a modern treaty port, it retains a large number of European-style buildings, and the villas of Badaguan are like a picturesque international architecture exhibition. Mount Lao is a famous mountain above the sea, with a long Daoist tradition. Qingdao is a romantic seaside city of red tiles, green trees, blue sea and clear sky. European architecture and colonial treaty-port culture left the Badaguan international architecture exhibition, Mount Lao's Daoist culture runs deep, and maritime fishing culture produced a distinctive seafood tradition. Every summer the Qingdao International Beer Festival is Asia's largest beer celebration, drawing visitors from around the world. The sea breeze at Zhanqiao, the malt aroma of beer and the sweetness of seafood together make Qingdao's distinctive city flavor.

Geography: Qingdao is surrounded by the sea on three sides and has a pleasant climate; its red tiles, green trees, blue sea and clear sky form a distinctive cityscape. It has a temperate monsoon climate with cool, pleasant summers and is a famous seaside resort. Qingdao is in Shandong, where landforms, rivers and climate shape the city's spatial pattern. Summer (July–September) is for a seaside escape and the beer festival; autumn (September–October) brings clear, crisp air and plump seafood. Natural and cultural sites are fairly concentrated, making one- or two-day itineraries by area practical. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons; check the weather and scenic-area notices before departure. Roads and railways are the main connections, and the main sights can be linked from the city or county town.

Highlights: Walk Zhanqiao Pier, wander Badaguan, climb Mount Lao and drink beer, experiencing the seaside style of red tiles, green trees, blue sea and clear sky. Core sights include Zhanqiao Pier, Badaguan, the Tsingtao Beer Museum and Mount Lao Scenic Area. They cover history, nature and city life and can be combined freely. Pair the main sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or nature as time allows. Local food is part of the trip — save a meal for a time-honoured shop or a local market.

Food Culture: Qingdao is known for beer and seafood: spicy stir-fried clams are sweet and juicy, mackerel dumplings have thin wrappers and generous filling, and sea-worm rice is rich and savory. Unfiltered draft beer with seafood is the most authentic combination.

Zhanqiao Pier

★★★★★Recommend 4.6 / 5

Zhanqiao is Qingdao's landmark, first built in the eighteenth year of the Qing Guangxu era (1892). It is 440 meters long, with the Huilan Pavilion at its southern end. Originally a military pier, it has become Qingdao's most famous tourist attraction. The pier reaches from the shore into the blue water, and its scenery changes with the seasons; it is the best place to view Qingdao's front sea. Qingdao's landmark, first built in the Guangxu era, has the Huilan Pavilion reaching into the bay like a pearl on the sea and is the starting point for watching the tide, listening to the waves and taking in the front sea. Highlights include the breakwater, views of the pier from the sea and the sunset over the front sea. Tip: go in the evening to enjoy the sunset and the night view of Huilan Pavilion. Practical info: located at 14 Taiping Road, Shinan District, Qingdao, Shandong; admission free; open all day. Best season: summer (July–September) for a seaside escape and the beer festival; autumn (September–October) for clear, crisp air and plump seafood. Combine with Badaguan, the Tsingtao Beer Museum and Mount Lao Scenic Area for a half- or full-day itinerary.

Tip: Go in the evening to enjoy the sunset and the night view of Huilan Pavilion.

  • Address: 14 Taiping Road, Shinan District, Qingdao, Shandong
  • Ticket: Free
  • Opening: Open all day

Badaguan

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Badaguan is one of Qingdao's most famous scenic areas. Eight roads named after passes of the Great Wall crisscross the district, which contains more than 200 European-style villas in the architectural styles of Russia, Britain, France, Germany, the United States and more than twenty other countries, earning it the name "international architecture exhibition." Each road is planted with a different species of tree, so the scenery changes with the seasons. Highlights include Huashi Villa, Princess Villa, the Song Family Garden and the peach blossoms on Shaoguan Road. The villa district named after the eight passes gathers Russian, British, French and other styles; Huashi Villa and the tree-lined avenues are scenic in every season, and it is known as an international architecture exhibition. Tip: the peach blossoms in spring and the golden leaves in autumn are the most beautiful. Practical info: located at Wushengguan Road, Shinan District, Qingdao, Shandong; admission free; open all day. Best season: summer (July–September) for a seaside escape and the beer festival; autumn (September–October) for clear, crisp air and plump seafood. Combine with Zhanqiao Pier, the Tsingtao Beer Museum and Mount Lao Scenic Area for a half- or full-day itinerary.

Tip: The peach blossoms in spring and the golden leaves in autumn are the most beautiful.

  • Address: Wushengguan Road, Shinan District, Qingdao, Shandong
  • Ticket: Free
  • Opening: Open all day

Tsingtao Beer Museum

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

The Tsingtao Beer Museum occupies the former site of the Germania Brewery Company Qingdao, built in 1903, and is China's first beer museum. It preserves the mashing workshop, fermentation tanks, copper equipment and old production line from a century ago, and displays the history of Tsingtao Beer from the recipes of German technicians to today's industrial production. The visit ends in the tasting area, and admission usually includes one glass each of unfiltered and draft beer. A beer museum in the old 1903 German brewery, it shows century-old mashing equipment, fermentation tanks and the earliest production line, and admission includes unfiltered and draft tastings — one of Qingdao's most characterful attractions. Highlights include the century-old mashing workshop, the old fermentation tanks and the Tsingtao Beer history exhibition. Tip: mornings are relatively quiet; admission includes alcohol, so do not drive. Practical info: located at 56 Dengzhou Road, Shibei District, Qingdao; ticket about ¥60 (includes tastings; subject to official notices); opening hours 08:30–17:30 (closing may be later in peak season).

Tip: Mornings are relatively quiet; admission includes alcohol, so do not drive.

  • Address: 56 Dengzhou Road, Shibei District, Qingdao
  • Ticket: About ¥60 (includes tastings; subject to official notices)
  • Opening: 08:30–17:30 (closing may be later in peak season)
