City Intro

History & Culture: Hefei, known in antiquity as Luzhou and Luyang, has a history spanning over two thousand years. It is a land of the Three Kingdoms, hometown of Bao Zheng, and cradle of the Huai Army. The Lord Bao Memorial (Baogong Ci) and Bao Zheng's tomb honor integrity, while Li Hongzhang's Former Residence showcases late Qing history. As Anhui's provincial capital, Hefei boasts formidable scientific and educational strength — the University of Science and Technology of China and "Science Island" are renowned nationwide, making it a major national base for science and education. Hefei is the hometown of Bao Zheng, and Bao Zheng culture has a long history: the integrity spirit of "a pure heart is the root of governance, an upright path is one's own design" is passed down from generation to generation. Luju is Hefei's local opera, with melodious and moving singing. Chaohu fishing songs reflect the life wisdom of the lake people. The Hefei Crayfish Festival is a food-culture event that has risen in recent years and attracts many diners every summer. As a national science and education base, Hefei brings together well-known institutions such as the University of Science and Technology of China, creating a strong academic atmosphere. Three Kingdoms culture, Huai Army culture and Bao Zheng culture interweave to form Hefei's distinctive cultural heritage.

Geography: Located in central Anhui on the shores of Chaohu Lake, Hefei takes its name from the confluence of the East Fei River and South Fei River. Chaohu Lake, one of China's five largest freshwater lakes, stretches vast and misty, with Laoshan Island dotting its waters. The Dabie Mountain foothills wind along the west, and the land is low in the southeast and high in the northwest. Hefei lies in Anhui, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer pleasant weather, ideal for visiting Chaohu Lake and ancient towns; summer can be hot. Natural and cultural sites are relatively concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary by area works well. Light, vegetation and water levels change the look of the landscape with the seasons; check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out.

Highlights: Visit the Lord Bao Memorial to learn about integrity culture, tour Sanhe Ancient Town for Hui-style architecture, admire the misty expanse of Chaohu Lake and explore the Anhui Provincial Museum to understand Jianghuai civilisation. Core sights include the Lord Bao Memorial (Bao Park), Sanhe Ancient Town, Chaohu Lake (Zhongmiao Temple · Laoshan Island) and the Anhui Provincial Museum. They cover history, nature and city life, and can be combined freely. Pair the main sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half- or full-day route. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest. First-time visitors can start with the most representative sights, then add museums, old streets or nature as time allows.

Food Culture: Luzhou cuisine emphasizes savory and fresh flavors. Luzhou roast duck has crispy skin and tender meat; Li Hongzhang's Hodgepodge combines meat and vegetables in harmony; Sanhe rice dumplings are crispy outside and tender inside; and Wushan tribute goose is savory and rich — all reflecting the hearty and honest character of Hefei's culinary traditions.

Lord Bao Memorial (Baogong Ci, Bao Park)

★★★★★Recommend 4.6 / 5

The Lord Bao Memorial is a hall commemorating Bao Zheng, the famous Northern Song official, and stands on the bank of the Baohe River. The site preserves Bao Zheng's tomb, Qingfeng Pavilion and other structures amid towering ancient trees in a quiet setting. Bao Zheng's integrity spirit — "a pure heart is the root of governance, an upright path is one's own design" — is passed on here. Highlights include Xiaosu Hall, Bao Zheng's tomb and the Baohe River scenery. Built in memory of Bao Zheng, the famous Northern Song official, it stands on the bank of the Baohe River, with Xiaosu Hall, Bao Zheng's tomb and Qingfeng Pavilion linked together amid lush old trees — a symbol of integrity culture and Luzhou heritage. Tip: hiring a guide to tell Bao Zheng's stories is recommended for a deeper understanding of integrity culture. Practical info: located at No. 72 Wuhu Road, Baohe District, Hefei, Anhui; admission ¥50; opening hours 08:00–17:30. Best season: spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer pleasant weather, ideal for visiting Chaohu Lake and ancient towns; summer can be hot. Pair it with Sanhe Ancient Town, Chaohu Lake (Zhongmiao Temple · Laoshan Island) and the Anhui Provincial Museum for a half- or full-day itinerary.

Tip: Hiring a guide to tell Bao Zheng's stories is recommended for a deeper appreciation of integrity culture.

  • Address: No. 72 Wuhu Road, Baohe District, Hefei City, Anhui
  • Ticket: ¥50
  • Opening: 08:00–17:30

Sanhe Ancient Town

★★★★★Recommend 4.5 / 5

Sanhe Ancient Town is about 40 km from Hefei and is a typical water-town settlement. It takes its name from the meeting of the Fengle, Hangbu and Xiaonan rivers; the old streets, bridges and dwellings are well preserved, and small bridges over flowing water, Hui-style architecture and Huai Army culture blend together. Highlights include the old streets and lanes, the Xiaonan River scenery, the Huai Army culture display and Sanhe rice dumplings. Tip: going in the morning is recommended, when you can taste authentic Sanhe rice dumplings. Practical info: located at Sanhe Town, Feixi County, Hefei, Anhui; admission free (combined tickets for some sights); open all day. Best season: spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer pleasant weather, ideal for visiting Chaohu Lake and ancient towns; summer can be hot. Pair it with the Lord Bao Memorial (Bao Park), Chaohu Lake (Zhongmiao Temple · Laoshan Island) and the Anhui Provincial Museum for a half- or full-day itinerary. Confirm same-day opening and reservation policies before you go and allow ample time. In peak season or on holidays, book ahead and travel off-peak, and allow time for queues and security checks.

Tip: It is recommended to visit in the morning and sample authentic Sanhe rice dumplings.

  • Address: Sanhe Town, Feixi County, Hefei City, Anhui
  • Ticket: Free (combo ticket for some sites)
  • Opening: Open all day

Chaohu Lake (Zhongmiao Temple · Laoshan Island)

★★★★☆Recommend 4.4 / 5

Chaohu Lake is one of China's five largest freshwater lakes, vast in area and stretching misty to the horizon. Laoshan Island stands in the lake, and its Wenfeng Pagoda can be climbed for distant views. Zhongmiao Temple, with thriving incense, is a thousand-year-old monastery on the lakeshore. Highlights include Wenfeng Pagoda on Laoshan Island, Chaohu Lake sunset and lake-fresh cuisine. Tip: visit on a clear day, when you can take a boat on the lake. Practical info: located at Zhongmiao Street, Chaohu City, Hefei, Anhui; admission about ¥60 for the Laoshan Island boat; opening hours 08:00–17:30. Best season: spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer pleasant weather, ideal for visiting Chaohu Lake and ancient towns; summer can be hot. Pair it with the Lord Bao Memorial (Bao Park), Sanhe Ancient Town and the Anhui Provincial Museum for a half- or full-day itinerary. Confirm same-day opening and reservation policies before you go and allow ample time.

Tip: It is recommended to visit on a clear day and take a boat tour.

  • Address: Zhongmiao Street, Chaohu City, Hefei City, Anhui
  • Ticket: Laoshan Island boat ticket approx. ¥60
  • Opening: 08:00–17:30

Culture

Hefei is the hometown of Bao Zheng, and the culture of integrity runs deep — "A pure heart is the root of governance; an upright path is one's life strategy" is passed down through generations. Luju opera is the local theatrical art, with melodious and moving arias. Chaohu fishermen's songs reflect the wisdom of lakeside life. The Hefei Crayfish Festival is a rising culinary cultural event that attracts food lovers every summer. As a national base for science and education, Hefei hosts prestigious institutions like the University of Science and Technology of China. Three Kingdoms culture, Huai Army culture, and Bao Zheng culture intertwine to form Hefei's unique cultural heritage.
