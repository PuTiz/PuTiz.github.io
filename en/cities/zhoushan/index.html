City Intro

History & Culture: Zhoushan is a fishing ground of the East China Sea and an important coastal defence town; Putuo Shan has been Guanyin's sacred site since the Tang dynasty, and its three great temples together with Nanhai Guanyin form one of China's four great Buddhist mountains. Putuo Shan is the sacred site of Guanyin Bodhisattva, and the three great temples of Puji, Fayu and Huiji together with the Nanhai Guanyin statue form a complete pilgrimage system; Zhoushan's fisher culture, Mazu worship and Buddhism coexist. Zhoushan is located in Zhejiang Province and has formed a distinctive urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, walls, lanes, temples and guild halls from different periods remain as historical layers. Today museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's historical thread legible.

Geography: The Zhoushan archipelago consists of more than 1,300 islands; Putuo Shan is one small island among them and is reached by boat from the Wugongzhi pier at Zhujiajian. Zhoushan is located in Zhejiang Province, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. Best April–June and September–November; summer brings typhoons and ferry services may be suspended, so check the weather before setting out. Natural and cultural sights are fairly concentrated, so a one- or two-day itinerary can be arranged by area. Light, vegetation and water levels change with the seasons; check the weather and official scenic-area notices before setting out. Roads and railways are the main connections, and the main sights can be linked from the city or county town.

Highlights: Make a pilgrimage to Putuo Shan's three great temples, see Nanhai Guanyin, go to Zhujiajian to see the sea, then eat a bowl of seafood noodles. Core sights include Puji Temple, Fayu Temple, Huiji Temple and Nanhai Guanyin. These sights cover different sides of history, nature and city life and can be combined freely according to interest. It is worth linking the core sights with nearby streets, museums or food streets for a half-day or full-day itinerary. If time allows, slow down and leave room for walking and rest for a fuller experience. First-time visitors can start with the most representative core sights, then add museums, old streets or natural areas as time allows. Local food is part of the itinerary too — save mealtime for a time-honoured shop or a local market.

Food Culture: Putuo vegetarian food, seafood noodles, Guanyin cakes and Zhoushan hairtail show island and monastic cooking side by side.

Puji Temple

Puji Temple

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

The first of Putuo Shan's three great temples, founded in the Song dynasty, with the Haiyin Pond and Imperial Stele Pavilion in front — the first stop on a pilgrimage. Highlights include the Great Yuantong Hall and the start of the pilgrimage. Tip: Walk or take the scenic shuttle from the pier to Puji Temple, see the Haiyin Pond first and then enter the halls; incense tickets are bought separately. Practical info: located at Putuo Shan, Putuo District, Zhoushan, Zhejiang; ticket included in the Putuo Shan entrance ticket (temple incense tickets extra); opening hours 06:00–18:00. Best season: April–June and September–November; summer brings typhoons and ferry services may be suspended, so check the weather before setting out. It can be combined with Fayu Temple, Huiji Temple and Nanhai Guanyin in Zhoushan for a half-day or full-day itinerary. Check the day's opening and booking policy before setting out, and allow plenty of time for the visit. In peak season or on holidays, book ahead, travel off-peak and allow time for queues and security checks. Plan your length of stay around the opening hours and your energy. If carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow extra time for security checks and walking.

  • Address: Putuo Shan, Putuo District, Zhoushan, Zhejiang
  • Ticket: Included in the Putuo Shan entrance ticket (temple incense tickets extra)
  • Opening: 06:00–18:00

Fayu Temple

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

Putuo Shan's second great temple; the Ming glazed tiles and nine-dragon caisson of its Nine Dragon Hall were moved here from the Ming palace in Nanjing, making it the island's finest building. Highlights include the Haihui Bridge. Tip: The Nine Dragon Hall is the key sight, with good light in the morning; scenic shuttles run between it and Puji Temple. Practical info: located at Putuo Shan, Putuo District, Zhoushan, Zhejiang; ticket included in the entrance ticket (incense tickets extra); opening hours 06:00–18:00. Huiji Temple

Huiji Temple

★★★★★Recommend 4.6 / 5

Putuo Shan's highest temple, on Buddha Summit; reach it by cable car or the pilgrim path, and from the top look down over Putuo Shan and the East China Sea. Highlights include the island panorama. Tip: Cable-car queues are long; if you are fit, walk up the pilgrim path; it is windy at the summit, so bring a jacket. Practical info: located at Buddha Summit, Putuo Shan, Putuo District, Zhoushan, Zhejiang; ticket included in the entrance ticket (incense tickets extra); opening hours 06:00–17:30.
