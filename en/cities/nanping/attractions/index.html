Tianyou Peak

Tianyou Peak

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Wuyi Mountain's most classic viewpoint, with 848 steps up to the summit and views over the Nine-Bend Stream and the Danxia peaks. Main highlights include the Nine-Bend Stream overlook and the cloud sea. Touring tip: climb in the early morning to see the cloud sea; the steps are steep, so bring water. Practical information: the scenic area is located in the Wuyishan Scenic Area, Wuyishan City, Nanping City, Fujian Province; ticket included in the Wuyishan ticket (sightseeing bus separate); opening hours 06:30–18:00. Best travel season: best March–May and September–November; Wuyi Mountain is rainy in spring, and bamboo-raft drifting is most comfortable in summer. It can be combined with the Nine-Bend Stream, the Dahongpao Scenic Area and Yixiantian in Nanping for a half-day or full-day itinerary. If carrying luggage or travelling with children, allow more time for security checks and walking. Before setting out, confirm that day's opening and reservation policies so that a temporary closure does not disrupt your itinerary. Best season: Best March–May and September–November; Wuyi Mountain is rainy in spring, and bamboo-raft drifting is most comfortable in summer.

Tip: Climb in the early morning to see the cloud sea; the steps are steep, so bring water.

Nine-Bend Stream

★★★★★Recommend 4.8 / 5

Drift by bamboo raft down the Nine-Bend Stream, with Danxia cliffs and tea gardens on both banks reflected in the water — Wuyi Mountain's most unmissable experience. Main highlights include Jade Maiden Peak and King Peak. Touring tip: the bamboo raft must be booked in advance, and tickets are hard to get in peak season; bring sun protection. Practical information: the scenic area is located in the Wuyishan Scenic Area, Wuyishan City, Nanping City, Fujian Province; ticket bamboo raft about ¥130 (separate); opening hours 07:00–17:00. Dahongpao Scenic Area

Dahongpao Scenic Area

★★★★☆Recommend 4.7 / 5

The core production area of Wuyi rock tea, with the mother Dahongpao bushes on the cliff face and tea-garden trails as the sights. Main highlights include the cliff face and tea culture. Touring tip: on the same route as Tianyou Peak; do not pick tea leaves. Yixiantian

Yixiantian

★★★★☆Recommend 4.6 / 5

A narrow fissure between Danxia cliff walls, only tens of centimetres wide at its narrowest, requiring you to turn sideways to pass. Main highlights include the exploration and the moss. Touring tip: larger visitors or those with claustrophobia should be cautious; slippery after rain.
