City Intro

History & Culture: Jinzhong lies in central Shanxi and is the home town of the Shanxi merchants; Pingyao Ancient City, the Qiao Family Compound and the Wang Family Compound together tell the story of Ming-Qing draft banks and courtyard-mansion culture. Jinzhong is the core area of Shanxi merchant culture: Pingyao's draft banks pioneered modern Chinese finance, while the Qiao and Wang compounds freeze family ethics and architectural art into the three carvings of brick, wood and stone. Jinzhong City lies in Shanxi Province and has formed a distinct urban memory through regional history and cultural exchange. From ancient settlements to the modern city, city walls, lanes, temples and guild halls record historical layers from different periods. Today, museums, historic districts and local festivals still make the city's historical thread legible. The city's history is written not only in sites and museums but also in the details of dialect, food, festivals and everyday life.

Geography: The Jinzhong basin lies between the Fen River and the Taihang Mountains, with old cities and compounds such as Pingyao, Qi County and Lingshi distributed along the merchant routes. Jinzhong City lies in Shanxi Province, where landforms, rivers and climate together shape the city's spatial pattern. April–October is best; autumn brings clear skies and crisp air. Pingyao Ancient City involves a lot of walking, so wear comfortable shoes. Natural and cultural landscapes within the city are fairly concentrated, making one- or two-day itineraries by area practical. Light, vegetation and water levels change the look of the landscape with the seasons; check the weather and scenic-area notices before setting out. Transport within the city is mainly by road and rail, and the main sights can be linked from the city or county towns. In mountain areas, watch altitude and changing weather; in river valleys and lake areas, watch water levels and flood season.

Highlights: Explore Pingyao Ancient City, see the three carvings at the Qiao Family Compound and the Wang Family Compound, and then eat a bowl of Pingyao beef. Food Culture: Pingyao beef, wantuo, oat kaolaolao and oil tea: wheat dishes and braised foods are part of everyday life in Jinzhong.

Pingyao Ancient City

★★★★★Recommend 4.9 / 5

Pingyao Ancient City was first built in the Western Zhou; the city wall was rebuilt in 1370, the third year of the Hongwu era of the Ming dynasty, and the surviving wall has a circuit of about 6.2 kilometres, making this China's most completely preserved Ming-Qing county town. Inside the city, the Rishengchang Draft Bank was China's first draft bank, and the county office, Confucian temple, City God Temple, escort agencies and Ming-Qing streets are completely preserved; it was inscribed on the World Cultural Heritage list in 1997. A World Cultural Heritage site and China's most completely preserved Ming-Qing county town, where the city walls, the Rishengchang Draft Bank, the county office and Ming-Qing streets form a complete Shanxi merchant city. Highlights include the ancient city wall and the Pingyao county office. Tip: the combo ticket covers the main sights in the city and needs at least a full day; cycling is allowed on the city wall, and for accommodation inside the city choose somewhere near South Street. Practical info: located at Pingyao County, Jinzhong, Shanxi; the ticket is ¥125 (old-town combo ticket, including the city walls, draft bank, county office and more); opening hours are 08:00–18:00. Best season: April–October is best; autumn brings clear skies and crisp air. Pingyao Ancient City involves a lot of walking, so wear comfortable shoes. It is advisable to plan the length of stay sensibly according to opening hours and your energy.

Qiao Family Compound

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

The Qiao Family Compound is in Dongguan Town, Qi County, and was the mansion of the family of the Qing dynasty Shanxi merchant Qiao Zhiyong; it was built during the Qianlong reign and expanded several times afterwards. The compound consists of 6 large courtyards, 20 small courtyards and 313 rooms, its plan forming the character for “double happiness”, with fine brick, wood and stone carvings and painted decoration; the film Raise the Red Lantern was shot here. The castle-like compound of the Qiao merchant family, with 6 large courtyards and 313 rooms; the brick and wood carvings and the furnishings of the “Zhongzhong Tang” hall represent Shanxi merchant culture. Highlights include the Qiao family fort. Tip: a hired car can cover it with Pingyao on the same day; hire a guide to see the details of the “three carvings”. Practical info: located at Dongguan Town, Qi County, Jinzhong, Shanxi; the ticket is ¥115 (subject to the scenic area's notices); opening hours are 08:00–18:00. Best season: April–October is best; autumn brings clear skies and crisp air. Pingyao Ancient City involves a lot of walking, so wear comfortable shoes. It can be combined with Pingyao Ancient City, Wang Family Compound and Shuanglin Temple in Jinzhong City for a half-day or one-day itinerary.

Wang Family Compound

★★★★★Recommend 4.7 / 5

The Wang Family Compound is in Jingsheng Town, Lingshi County, and is the residential complex of the Wang family in the Qing dynasty, made up of five lanes, six forts and one street, with 123 surviving courtyards and 1,118 rooms. The buildings rise along the mountain, and the brick, wood and stone carvings draw on a rich repertoire of subjects; it is hailed as “the finest residential mansion in China” and is the largest of the Shanxi merchant compounds. The Wang family compound in Jingsheng Town, Lingshi County, billed as “the finest residential mansion in China”, with five lanes, six forts and one street, and brick, wood and stone carvings on an astounding scale. Highlights include Gaojiaya, Hongmen Fort and the art of the three carvings. Tip: combining it with Pingyao and the Qiao compound on the same day is rushed, so allow a separate half day; hire a guide to see the three carvings.
